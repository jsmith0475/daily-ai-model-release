# AI Model Releases - June 26, 2026

### Top Updates

- **Claude Fable 5 — Anthropic** *(Released June 9, 2026)*
  Anthropic's flagship model achieved a **95% SWE-bench score**, shattering previous records and representing a significant leap in AI coding capability. Fable 5 sets a new frontier for agentic software engineering tasks.

- **Gemini 3.5 Pro — Google DeepMind** *(GA, June 2026)*
  Features a massive **2-million-token context window** and a specialized "Deep Think" cognitive mode for extended reasoning. Positioned as Google's most capable frontier model, announced at Google I/O 2026 alongside Gemini 3.5 Flash.

- **Gemini 3.5 Flash — Google DeepMind** *(GA May 19, 2026)*
  Ultra-fast inference model that became the default powering all Gemini consumer products. Prioritizes speed and cost-efficiency while maintaining strong multimodal capabilities.

- **Gemini 3.1 Flash Image & Gemini 3 Pro Image — Google** *(Released June 2026)*
  Specialized image-generation models released via Google AI Studio. Extend Google's multimodal lineup with dedicated image synthesis capabilities.

- **MAI Model Family (7 models) — Microsoft AI** *(Announced June 2026 at Build)*
  Microsoft unveiled seven in-house models under the MAI banner:
  - **MAI-Thinking-1**: Flagship reasoning model matching leading models on SWE-bench and advanced math.
  - **MAI-Code-1-Flash**: 5B active-parameter agentic coding model optimized for GitHub Copilot and VS Code.
  - **MAI Transcribe-1.5**: Described as the world's best transcription model.

- **Apple Core AI / Apple Foundation Models (AFM) — Apple** *(Announced WWDC June 2026)*
  Apple introduced a full on-device AI stack for developers, including the Core AI framework. Siri was rebuilt on a custom **1.2-trillion-parameter Gemini model** licensed from Google, with Claude added as a first-class iPhone AI option via multi-AI Extensions.

- **GLM-5.2 — Z-ai (Zhipu AI)** *(Released June 2026)*
  Open-source large language model release from Zhipu AI, continuing the expansion of capable open-weight alternatives to proprietary frontier models.

- **step-3.7-flash — StepFun** *(Released June 2026)*
  Efficient inference-focused model from StepFun targeting fast, low-cost deployment use cases.

- **RTX Spark Superchip — NVIDIA** *(Announced June 2026)*
  NVIDIA announced the RTX Spark, a new consumer-grade AI superchip co-developed with Microsoft, aimed at bringing on-device AI inference to everyday PCs and "reinventing the laptop."

---

### Key Trends (June 2026)

- Reasoning models trading speed for accuracy are now standard across all frontier providers.
- Multimodal capabilities (text + image + audio) are table-stakes at the frontier.
- On-device / edge AI seeing major investment from Apple and NVIDIA.
- Efficiency gains delivering GPT-4-class performance at dramatically reduced costs.
