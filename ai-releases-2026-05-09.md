## AI Model Releases - May 9, 2026

### Top Updates

- **GPT-5.5 & GPT-5.5 Cyber (OpenAI)** — GPT-5.5, shipped April 23, pushes deeper into coding and agentic workflows; GPT-5.5 Instant (May 5) is billed as smarter and more personalized. OpenAI also rolled out GPT-5.5 Cyber on May 7 — a security-focused variant made available to vetted cybersecurity teams, arriving roughly a month after Anthropic's Mythos debut.
  - Key specs: improved long-context reasoning; exact parameter count undisclosed
  - Status: Released (GPT-5.5 / Instant), Limited beta (Cyber)

- **Gemini 3.1 Ultra (Google)** — Google's most significant model release of the year, featuring a 2-million token context window with fully native multimodal processing across text, image, audio, and video — no transcription intermediaries required.
  - Key specs: 2M-token context window, native multimodal
  - Status: Released

- **Claude Opus 4.7 (Anthropic)** — Latest Opus-tier model from Anthropic, noted for safer, more literal outputs and continued strength on complex reasoning tasks. Anthropic also announced a new enterprise AI services company (with Blackstone, H&F, and Goldman Sachs) to bring Claude into mid-market operations.
  - Status: Released

- **Grok 4.1 (xAI)** — xAI's newest Grok model combining high emotional intelligence with strong logical reasoning, currently topping the LM Arena leaderboard. Oracle Cloud Infrastructure also made Grok 4.3 available via OCI Enterprise AI.
  - Status: Released

- **Meta Muse Spark (Meta)** — Meta's first flagship LLM built under Chief AI Officer Alexandr Wang's newly formed Superintelligence Labs. Competitive on multimodal perception, reasoning, health, and agentic tasks at a fraction of the compute cost of Llama 4 mid-size.
  - Status: Released / Announced

- **Kimi K2 Thinking (Moonshot AI)** — Open-weights reasoning model from Moonshot AI setting new records on reasoning benchmarks. Part of a wave of Chinese open-weights releases: Z.ai GLM-5.1, MiniMax M2.7, and DeepSeek V4 all shipped within a 12-day window.
  - Status: Released (open weights)

- **DeepSeek V4 (DeepSeek)** — Competitive on price and long-context tasks; open weights. Part of a broader Chinese lab push in coding-focused open models alongside GLM-5.1, MiniMax M2.7, and Kimi K2.6.
  - Status: Released (open weights)

- **NVIDIA Ising (NVIDIA)** — World's first family of open-source AI models purpose-built to accelerate quantum computing simulation and optimization workloads.
  - Status: Released (open source)
