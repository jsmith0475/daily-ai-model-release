## AI Model Releases - June 18, 2026

### Top Updates

- **Microsoft MAI Family (7 models) — Microsoft AI**
  Microsoft launched a family of seven in-house AI models, reducing reliance on OpenAI. Highlights include **MAI-Thinking-1** (flagship reasoning model that matches leading models on software engineering benchmarks and is preferred over Sonnet 4.6 in blind human evals), **MAI-Code-1-Flash** (5B active-parameter agentic coding model built for GitHub Copilot/VS Code), and **MAI Transcribe-1.5** (claimed state-of-the-art transcription accuracy).
  Availability: Released

- **Claude Fable 5 — Anthropic**
  Anthropic released Claude Fable 5 on June 9, 2026, marking a major leap in coding, multidisciplinary reasoning, and agentic computer-use tasks. Follows the Claude Opus 4.8 launch which also shipped recently with significant capability upgrades.
  Availability: Released

- **Gemini 3.5 Flash — Google**
  Shipped at Google I/O 2026 (May 19) and now the default model powering both the Gemini app and Google Search AI Mode, replacing the prior AI Overviews experience. Positioned as a fast, cost-efficient frontier model for high-volume consumer deployments.
  Availability: Released (GA)

- **Gemini 3.1 Ultra — Google**
  Features a 2-million token context window with native multimodal understanding across text, image, audio, and video — no transcription intermediaries required. Targeted at enterprise and research workloads requiring long-context reasoning.
  Key specs: 2M token context, natively multimodal
  Availability: Released

- **MiniMax M3 — MiniMax**
  Open-weight model with a 1M-token context window and native multimodality. Represents the closing US–China AI capability gap, with M3 benchmarking on par with Western flagship models while dramatically lowering inference costs.
  Key specs: 1M context, open-weight, natively multimodal
  Availability: Released (open-weight)

- **Meta Muse Spark — Meta**
  Meta's first flagship large language model (distinct from the Llama research line). Delivers competitive performance on multimodal perception, reasoning, health, and agentic tasks at significantly lower compute cost than Llama 4 mid-size variants.
  Availability: Released

- **Gemini-Powered Siri — Apple / Google**
  At Tim Cook's final keynote as Apple CEO, Apple announced Siri is now powered by a custom 1.2-trillion-parameter Gemini model licensed from Google (~$1B/year deal). Claude is also available as an iPhone AI option for the first time via a new multi-AI Extensions system.
  Key specs: 1.2T-parameter Gemini model
  Availability: Announced

- **OpenAI Real-Time Audio Models (3 models) — OpenAI**
  OpenAI launched three new real-time audio models targeting conversational agents, translation, and transcription use cases. Additionally, OpenAI introduced **LifeSciBench** (June 17) for life-science reasoning evaluation and **Deployment Simulation**, a method that replays past conversations through candidate models before release.
  Availability: Released
