# AI Model Releases - April 25, 2026

### Top Updates

- **DeepSeek V4 (DeepSeek)** — Released April 24 as two variants: V4-Pro (high-capability) and V4-Flash (faster, lower-cost). Introduces a Hybrid Attention Architecture for improved long-context memory and pushes a 1-million token context window. Claims top-tier performance on coding benchmarks with notable gains in reasoning and agentic tasks.
  - *Status: Released (preview)*

- **GPT-5.5 (OpenAI)** — Released April 23. Described as OpenAI's "smartest and most intuitive" model to date, targeting enterprise use cases including agentic coding, knowledge work, mathematics, and scientific research. Positioned as a step toward a unified AI "super app."
  - *Status: Released*

- **Gemini 3.1 Ultra (Google)** — Features a 2-million token context window with native multimodal reasoning across text, image, audio, and video simultaneously — no transcription intermediaries. Designed to reason across modalities from training, not post-hoc.
  - *Status: Released*

- **Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite (Google)** — Efficiency-focused variant delivering 2.5× faster response times and 45% faster output generation vs. prior Gemini versions. Priced at $0.25 per million input tokens.
  - *Status: Released*

- **ChatGPT Images 2.0 (OpenAI)** — Launched April 21. Major upgrade to image generation with improved text rendering, stronger visual reasoning, and greater precision and control.
  - *Status: Released*

- **Muse Spark (Meta)** — Meta's first flagship LLM developed under Chief AI Officer Alexandr Wang's newly formed Superintelligence Labs. Specifics on specs/benchmarks not yet fully disclosed.
  - *Status: Announced/Released*

- **Ising (NVIDIA)** — World's first family of open-source AI models purpose-built to accelerate quantum computing. Delivers up to 2.5× faster and 3× more accurate quantum error-correction decoding compared to traditional approaches.
  - *Status: Released*

- **Claude Opus 4.7 with Routines (Anthropic)** — Claude can now be scheduled to run complex multi-step tasks autonomously on Anthropic's servers. Also announced: Claude Design, a generative tool that builds full presentations, websites, brand videos, and apps from a single text prompt.
  - *Status: Announced*
