# AI Model Releases - 2026-05-08

### Top Updates

- **Google Gemini 3.1 Ultra (Google)** — The most significant Google model release of 2026, featuring a native 2-million token context window across text, image, audio, and video without transcription intermediaries. Tops major benchmarks; now available for developers.

- **Google Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite (Google)** — Lightweight variant delivering 2.5× faster response times and 45% faster output generation. Priced at $0.25 per million input tokens, making it one of the most cost-efficient frontier models available.

- **Meta Muse Spark (Meta)** — Meta's first flagship LLM from its newly formed Superintelligence Labs (led by Chief AI Officer Alexandr Wang). Competitive on multimodal perception, reasoning, health, and agentic tasks at a fraction of the compute cost of Llama 4 mid-size. Released.

- **xAI Grok 4.1 (xAI)** — Latest Grok release combining high EQ with strong logic, currently topping the LM Arena leaderboard. Released.

- **Moonshot Kimi K2 Thinking (Moonshot AI)** — Open-weights reasoning model setting new records on reasoning benchmarks. Released open-source.

- **Anthropic Claude Opus 4.5 (Anthropic)** — Elite coding and agentic performance at a significantly reduced price vs. prior Opus tiers. Also notable: the Claude Mythos Preview cleared a 32-step end-to-end cyber-attack range, flagging new frontier capability milestones. Released.

- **Anthropic Managed Agents Platform (Anthropic)** — Entered public beta on May 6, 2026. Roadzen Inc. disclosed as a beta testing partner. Platform enables enterprise agentic deployments with Claude. Beta.

- **Chinese Open-Weights Coding Quartet** — Four Chinese labs dropped open-weights coding models simultaneously: Z.ai **GLM-5.1**, MiniMax **M2.7**, Moonshot **Kimi K2.6**, and **DeepSeek V4** — all offering competitive pricing vs. Western counterparts. Released open-source.

- **OpenAI GPT-5.5 / GPT 5.1 (OpenAI)** — GPT-5.5 Instant released; GPT 5.1 adds dedicated "Thinking" and "Instant" modes with an expanded context window. Preview available on Amazon Bedrock.

- **NVIDIA Ising (NVIDIA)** — World's first family of open-source AI models purpose-built to accelerate quantum computing, targeting quantum error correction and processor calibration. Announced.
