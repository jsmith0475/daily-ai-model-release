# AI Model Releases - 2026-05-20

## Top Updates

- **Gemini 3.5 Flash – Google**
  Now generally available (announced at Google I/O 2026, May 19–20). Delivers frontier-level intelligence at 4× the speed of comparable models, outperforming larger models on coding and reasoning benchmarks. Pricing: $1.50 / $9 per 1M input/output tokens; 1M-token context window. Powers the redesigned Google Search AI Mode and the Gemini app.

- **Gemini Spark – Google**
  Announced at Google I/O 2026. A new general-purpose AI agent that can reason across information in connected apps (Gmail, Drive, Calendar, etc.). Currently in beta; rolling out first to Google AI Ultra subscribers starting next week.

- **Omni (world model) – Google**
  Unveiled at Google I/O 2026. A world model designed to simulate physical environments with image and audio support. Integrated into Gemini Flash, the Gemini App, Google Flow, and YouTube Shorts. Availability: announced, limited preview.

- **NVIDIA Ising – NVIDIA**
  The world's first family of open-source AI models purpose-built to accelerate quantum computing research. Designed to bridge classical AI and quantum hardware simulation. Availability: open-source release.

- **AMD Ryzen AI 400 Series – AMD**
  New laptop processor family featuring an upgraded Neural Processing Unit (NPU) purpose-built to accelerate local on-device AI inference tasks. Availability: announced.

- **GPT-5.5 Instant – OpenAI**
  Became the default ChatGPT model on May 5, 2026 (six weeks after GPT-5.4). API access continuing to roll out to enterprise customers. Optimized for low-latency, high-throughput use cases.

- **SubQ 12M-Context LLM – SubQ**
  First commercial subquadratic large language model, supporting a 12M-token context window. Ships without the quadratic attention scaling bottleneck of transformer models. Status: preview (launched May 5, 2026).

- **Mistral 128B + Le Chat Work Mode – Mistral AI**
  Mistral's 128B flagship model launched May 3, 2026, paired with async cloud coding sessions and a new "Work" agentic mode in the Le Chat interface. Targets enterprise coding and multi-step agentic workflows.
