# AI Model Releases - April 27, 2026

### Top Updates

- **DeepSeek V4 Pro & V4 Flash (DeepSeek)** — Released April 24 as the latest flagship open-source challenge to OpenAI and Anthropic. V4 Pro features 1.6 trillion parameters with a native 1-million-token context window using only ~27% of the compute of prior models; V4 Flash comes in at 284 billion parameters for efficient deployment. Tops coding benchmarks with major advances in reasoning and agentic tasks. Status: **Released (preview)**

- **GPT-5.5 (OpenAI)** — Released April 23, six weeks after GPT-5.4. Tops the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at 60 points, with dominant performance on long-context and coding benchmarks. Described as OpenAI's "smartest and most intuitive" model yet, with stronger computer use and deep research capabilities. Rolling out to Plus, Pro, Business, and Enterprise subscribers. Status: **Released**

- **Gemini 3.1 Ultra (Google DeepMind)** — Most significant Google model release of the year; features a 2-million-token context window with native multimodal support across text, image, audio, and video (no transcription intermediaries). Also adds real-time voice and image analysis. Status: **Released**

- **Meta Muse Spark (Meta)** — Meta's first flagship LLM developed under Chief AI Officer Alexandr Wang's Superintelligence Labs. Competitive performance on multimodal perception, reasoning, health, and agentic tasks at a fraction of typical compute cost. Status: **Announced / Released**

- **NVIDIA Ising (NVIDIA)** — World's first family of open-source AI models purpose-built to accelerate quantum computing research and simulation. Unveiled at GTC 2026 alongside NeMoCLAW and OpenCLAW enterprise agentic deployment frameworks. Status: **Announced**

- **Claude Opus 4.7 (Anthropic)** — Released April 16; Anthropic's newest flagship aimed at complex reasoning and long-running agentic workflows. Status: **Released**

- **Gemma 4 (Google)** — Released April 2 under the Apache 2.0 license with no commercial restrictions. Delivers frontier-level intelligence built on the same research as Gemini 3.1 Pro. Status: **Released (open-source)**

- **Luma Agents / Uni-1 (Luma AI)** — New product coordinating multiple AI systems to generate end-to-end creative work across text, images, video, and audio, built on Luma's Uni-1 model. Status: **Launched**
