# AI Model Releases - July 13, 2026

> Compiled from web sources. Coverage window: ~July 8–13, 2026.

## Top Updates

- **OpenAI GPT-5.6 (Sol / Terra / Luna)** — OpenAI launched a three-model lineup on July 9, 2026, replacing single-model releases with a tiered lineup. *Sol* targets high-end reasoning, coding, and science ($5.00 / $30.00 per 1M tokens); *Terra* delivers GPT-5.5-level quality at roughly half the cost; *Luna* is optimized for speed and high-volume, lower-cost work. All three are now the preferred models inside Microsoft 365 Copilot.

- **xAI Grok 4.5** — Released July 8, 2026. Built specifically for coding and agentic workflows using real Cursor developer session data. Ships with a 500k-token context window (down from Grok 4.3's 1M), priced at $2 / $6 per 1M tokens. Scored the largest single-generation jump (+16 points) on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, landing 4th overall with 54 points.

- **Meta Muse Spark 1.1** — Released July 9, 2026. Targets agent-first use cases: ranked #1 on JobBench and MCP Atlas (professional and scaled tool-use benchmarks), supports zero-shot MCP generalization, multi-agent coordination, and built-in web search. Context window: 1M tokens. Pricing: $1.25 / $4.25 per 1M tokens. Compatible with both OpenAI and Anthropic SDKs out of the box.

- **Anthropic Claude Fable 5** — Returned to global availability on July 1, 2026 after a 19-day pause. Now accessible via Claude.ai, the Claude Platform API, Claude Code, and Claude Cowork on all paid plans. Anthropic announced extended access + 50% higher Claude Code weekly rate limits through July 19.

- **Z.ai GLM-5.2** — Released June 12, 2026 by Z.ai (Zhipu AI). Described as competitive with leading frontier models from Anthropic and OpenAI, signaling continued progress in Chinese AI development. Limited detailed specs available publicly.

- **NVIDIA nvDock** — Released July 8, 2026. Limited public information; tracked as a new NVIDIA model release. Likely targeting developer or inference workloads given NVIDIA's recent tooling focus.

- **Microsoft HARC-Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct** — Recent release from Microsoft based on the Qwen2.5-7B base, targeting efficient instruction-following at smaller scale. Part of a broader trend toward fine-tuned, task-specific smaller models.

- **NAVER HyperCLOVA X (infrastructure expansion)** — NAVER and NVIDIA announced a major expansion of sovereign AI infrastructure using the NVIDIA DSX platform: starting at 55 MW at the GAK Sejong data center (South Korea), with plans to scale to gigawatt capacity. Underpins next-generation HyperCLOVA X training runs.

## Context / Trends

- New AI models are now arriving roughly every **3 days** across major organizations.
- The competitive axis is shifting from "best benchmark wins" to **price, speed, and fit** — tiered lineups (Sol/Terra/Luna) and specialized models (Grok 4.5 for coding, Muse Spark 1.1 for agents) reflect this.
- **Cloudflare** launched granular AI bot management, letting site owners separately control Search, Agent, and Training crawlers — a sign that AI web access is becoming an ops concern.
