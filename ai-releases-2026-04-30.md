# AI Model Releases - April 30, 2026

### Top Updates

- **GPT-5.5 (OpenAI)** — OpenAI's first fully retrained foundation model since GPT-4.5, launched April 23, 2026. Excels at coding, computer use, and deep research; immediately claimed the #1 spot on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index with a record score of 60. Available now via ChatGPT and the API.

- **DeepSeek V4 Flash & V4 Pro (DeepSeek)** — Chinese AI lab DeepSeek unveiled preview versions of its newest flagship model on April 24, 2026. Supports a 1-million-token context window (allowing entire codebases as a single prompt), delivers top-tier coding benchmark results, and matches closed-source rivals from OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google — while remaining open source.

- **Google Gemini 3.1 Ultra (Google DeepMind)** — Google's most significant model release of 2026 features a native 2-million-token context window with true multimodal processing across text, image, audio, and video without transcription intermediaries. Available to Gemini Advanced subscribers.

- **Meta Muse Spark (Meta Superintelligence Labs)** — Meta's first flagship LLM built under Chief AI Officer Alexandr Wang's newly formed Superintelligence Labs. Competitive on multimodal perception, reasoning, health benchmarks, and agentic tasks at significantly lower compute cost than Llama 4 mid-size variants. Availability: announced, limited access.

- **Google Gemma 4 (Google)** — Released April 2, 2026, under the Apache 2.0 license. Delivers frontier-level intelligence without the hardware requirements of larger closed models; targets researchers and on-device deployments.

- **NVIDIA Ising (NVIDIA)** — The world's first family of open-source AI models purpose-built for quantum computing, targeting the two biggest barriers to practical quantum: error correction and processor calibration. Released as open source.

- **Luma Agents / Uni-1 (Luma AI)** — A multi-agent creative system built on Luma's Uni-1 foundation model, coordinating AI across text, images, video, and audio to produce end-to-end creative work. Aimed at creative and media production workflows. Available in beta.

- **Huawei 950PR AI Chip (Huawei)** — Huawei rolled out its 950PR, an inference-focused AI accelerator chip designed to reduce reliance on NVIDIA hardware for large-scale AI deployment.

---

### Notable: Not Released

- **Anthropic Claude Mythos** — A data leak confirmed Anthropic's most powerful model to date, but on April 7 Anthropic announced it will **not** be publicly released due to cybersecurity risk assessments. Access is restricted to select research partners under the internal **Project Glasswing** program.
