# AI Model Releases - 2026-05-07

### Top Updates

- **GPT-5.5 Instant — OpenAI**
  Replaces GPT-5.3 Instant as the default model powering ChatGPT. Released May 5, 2026, just six weeks after GPT-5.4, continuing OpenAI's accelerated shipping cadence.
  *Availability: Released*

- **Gemini 3.1 Ultra — Google**
  Flagship multimodal model with a 2-million token context window, processing text, image, audio, and video natively without transcription intermediaries.
  *Availability: Released*

- **Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite — Google**
  Lightweight model delivering 2.5× faster response times and 45% faster output generation versus earlier Gemini versions.
  Key specs: $0.25 per million input tokens.
  *Availability: Released*

- **Muse Spark — Meta**
  Meta's first flagship LLM from its newly formed Superintelligence Labs (led by Chief AI Officer Alexandr Wang). Targets competitive performance across multimodal perception, reasoning, health, and agentic tasks.
  *Availability: Announced / Released*

- **Claude Mythos Preview — Anthropic (Project Glasswing)**
  Anthropic's unreleased frontier model made available in limited preview to select partners including AWS, Apple, Cisco, Google, JPMorgan Chase, and Microsoft.
  *Availability: Limited preview*

- **Chinese Open-Weights Coding Quartet — Z.ai / MiniMax / Moonshot AI / DeepSeek**
  Four Chinese labs released open-weights coding models within a 12-day window: **GLM-5.1** (Z.ai), **M2.7** (MiniMax), **Kimi K2.6** (Moonshot), and **DeepSeek V4**. All reach roughly the same capability ceiling on agentic engineering benchmarks as Western frontier models at significantly lower inference cost.
  *Availability: Open-weights / Released*

- **Kimi K2 Thinking — Moonshot AI**
  Open reasoning model setting new records on reasoning benchmarks. Complements the Kimi K2.6 coding release.
  *Availability: Released*

- **Ising — NVIDIA**
  The world's first family of open-source AI models purpose-built to accelerate quantum computing. Marks a significant convergence of AI and quantum computing into a single open development stack.
  *Availability: Released*
