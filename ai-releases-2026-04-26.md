# AI Model Releases - April 26, 2026

## Top Updates

- **GPT-5.5 — OpenAI**
  Announced April 23, GPT-5.5 brings improved capabilities in coding, computer use, and deep research. Rolling out now to ChatGPT Plus, Pro, Business, and Enterprise subscribers, as well as the Codex coding assistant.
  *Status: Released (paid tiers)*

- **DeepSeek V4 (V4-Pro & V4-Flash) — DeepSeek**
  Released April 24, DeepSeek V4 is a 1-trillion-parameter Mixture-of-Experts model with fully open weights (Apache 2.0). V4-Pro targets demanding tasks; V4-Flash prioritizes speed and cost. Supports a 1-million-token context window and was trained on Huawei Ascend 950PR chips for an estimated $5.2M — competitive with US frontier models.
  *Status: Released (open-source)*

- **Gemini 3.1 Ultra — Google DeepMind**
  Features a 2-million-token context window with native multimodal reasoning across text, image, audio, and video — no transcription intermediaries. Ships with a sandboxed Code Execution tool and real-time voice and image analysis.
  *Status: Released*

- **Muse Spark — Meta**
  Meta's first flagship LLM built under Superintelligence Labs (Chief AI Officer Alexandr Wang). Delivers competitive performance on multimodal perception, reasoning, health, and agentic tasks at reduced compute cost. Meta also announced $115–135B in AI capex for 2026.
  *Status: Released*

- **Claude Opus (new generation) — Anthropic**
  Anthropic launched an updated Opus model with stronger performance across coding, agentic tasks, vision, and multi-step reasoning. Also announced Claude Design, a new visual collaboration product under Anthropic Labs.
  *Status: Released*

- **Ising — NVIDIA**
  The world's first family of open-source AI models purpose-built to accelerate quantum computing. Delivers up to 2.5× faster and 3× more accurate error-correction decoding vs. traditional approaches. Announced April 14 at GTC 2026.
  *Status: Released (open-source)*

- **Gemma 4 — Google**
  A family of four open models released April 2 under Apache 2.0, purpose-built for different deployment scenarios from edge to cloud.
  *Status: Released (open-source)*

- **Luma Agents — Luma AI**
  Built on Luma's Uni-1 model, Luma Agents coordinates multiple AI systems to generate end-to-end creative work across text, images, video, and audio in a single pipeline.
  *Status: Released*
