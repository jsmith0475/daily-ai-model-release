# AI Model Releases - 2026-07-01

## Top Updates

- **OpenAI GPT-5.6 Sol / Terra / Luna** — OpenAI launched GPT-5.6 under a new three-tier naming system (Sol = highest capability, Terra = mid, Luna = efficient), with Sol targeting advanced coding, scientific reasoning, long-horizon planning, and agentic workflows. Sol Ultra set a new Terminal-Bench 2.1 record of 91.9% (vs GPT-5.5 at 83.4%). Sol launches on Cerebras at up to 750 tokens/second in July 2026 for select customers.
  - Pricing (per 1M tokens): Sol $5 in / $30 out · Terra $2.50 in / $15 out · Luna $1 in / $6 out
  - Status: **Released / Rolling out**

- **Microsoft MAI Model Family (7 models)** — Microsoft AI unveiled seven in-house models reducing reliance on OpenAI. Highlights include:
  - *MAI-Thinking-1*: flagship reasoning model matching leading models on software engineering benchmarks and advanced math
  - *MAI-Code-1-Flash*: inference-efficient agentic coding model built for GitHub Copilot and VS Code
  - *MAI-Image-2.5*: text-to-image generation and image editing
  - *MAI Transcribe-1.5*: claimed state-of-the-art transcription accuracy across languages
  - Status: **Announced / Launching**

- **Google Gemini 3.5 Pro** — Slipped its Google I/O June deadline; now targeting July 2026 GA. Confirmed specs: 2-million-token context window, Deep Think reasoning mode (gated to Ultra subscribers at $250/month), frontier multimodal capability across text, image, audio, and video.
  - Status: **Announced / Coming soon**

- **OpenAI Speech-to-Text (ChatGPT Dictation)** — New speech-to-text model rolling out for dictation in ChatGPT across all plans. Improves transcription accuracy across languages and accents, including code-switching between languages.
  - Status: **Released**

- **OpenAI GeneBench-Pro** — Research-level benchmark for evaluating AI agents on computational biology tasks. Expands the original GeneBench with harder synthetic tasks and open-sourced representative questions; reports strong frontier model results on scientific reasoning under uncertainty.
  - Status: **Released (benchmark/research tool)**

- **Microsoft + Mayo Clinic Healthcare AI** — Microsoft and Mayo Clinic announced a collaboration to co-create a frontier AI model purpose-built for healthcare, targeting clinical reasoning and medical workflows.
  - Status: **Announced**

- **Microsoft 365 Copilot SMB SKUs** — Starting July 1, 2026, Microsoft 365 Business Standard with Copilot and Microsoft 365 Business Premium with Copilot become permanent product SKUs, expanding AI assistant availability to small and medium-sized businesses.
  - Status: **Live as of today**
