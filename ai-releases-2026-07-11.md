# AI Model Releases - July 11, 2026

> Daily briefing covering the latest AI model launches, updates, and announcements from the past 24–48 hours.

## Top Updates

- **GPT-5.6 (OpenAI)** — OpenAI's new three-tier model family (Luna, Terra, Sol) launched publicly on July 9, 2026 after a gated government-vetted preview. Sol is the most capable variant, optimized for long-horizon agentic and security tasks with 54% better token efficiency on coding workloads. Pricing: Sol $5/$30, Terra $2.50/$15, Luna $1/$6 per million input/output tokens. *Status: Generally available.*

- **Grok 4.5 (xAI)** — Released July 8, 2026. xAI's first model built specifically for coding and agentic workflows, trained on real Cursor developer session data. Scores 54 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index (4th out of 168 models, behind Claude Fable 5, GPT-5.5, and Claude Opus 4.8). API pricing: $2/$6 per million input/output tokens. *Status: Generally available via API.*

- **Claude Sonnet 5 (Anthropic)** — Launched June 30, 2026. Achieves 63.2% on SWE-Bench Pro, making it the most capable affordable Claude coder. Features a 1M-token context window and introductory pricing of $2/$10 per million input/output tokens. Best-in-class for writing style, voice fidelity, and complex instruction-following. *Status: Generally available.*

- **Claude Fable 5 (Anthropic)** — Currently the top-ranked model on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index as of July 2026. Usage-credit billing applies to all subscriber tiers starting July 8, 2026 at $10/$50 per million input/output tokens. *Status: Available (paid).*

- **Gemini 3.5 Pro (Google DeepMind)** — Cleared for general-availability launch in July 2026 after slipping its June target. Still in limited Vertex AI enterprise preview as of July 8; no confirmed public launch date yet. Gemini 3.1 Pro remains the top reasoning benchmark holder pending this release. *Status: Limited preview — GA expected mid-to-late July.*

- **GPT-5.6 Sol — Security Focus** — A notable capability highlight: GPT-5.6 Sol is OpenAI's most capable model for cybersecurity tasks, covering vulnerability research and exploitation scenarios. This was a key factor in the U.S. government's initial restricted release through the Department of Commerce's Center for AI Standards and Innovation (CAISI).

- **JADEPUFFER (Threat Actor Report)** — Security researchers disclosed the first confirmed fully autonomous AI ransomware attack, deploying 600 distinct payloads with no human at keyboard. Not a model release but a significant AI safety development this week.

- **Chinese AI Enterprise Adoption** — Chinese AI models now account for 30–46% of enterprise API token usage on U.S. developer platforms, with their share above 30% of all OpenRouter gateway tokens every week since February 2026.
