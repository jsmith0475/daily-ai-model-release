# AI Model Releases - 2026-05-04

## Top Updates

- **Google Gemini 3.1 Ultra** (Google)
  Google's most significant model release of the year. Features a 2-million token context window with native multimodal understanding across text, image, audio, and video — no transcription intermediaries required.
  *Status: Released*

- **OpenAI GPT-5.5** (OpenAI)
  Released April 23, 2026 — only six weeks after GPT-5.4. Stronger at coding, computer use, and deep research tasks. Rolling out to Plus, Pro, Business, and Enterprise subscribers via ChatGPT and Codex.
  *Status: Released (paid tiers)*

- **Meta Muse Spark** (Meta / Superintelligence Labs)
  Meta's first flagship LLM built under Chief AI Officer Alexandr Wang's Superintelligence Labs. Competitive on multimodal perception, reasoning, health, and agentic tasks at significantly lower compute cost than Llama 4 mid-size.
  *Status: Released (limited)*

- **DeepSeek V4 Flash & V4 Pro** (DeepSeek)
  The latest flagship series from the Chinese startup, unveiled roughly one year after DeepSeek upended Silicon Valley. Top-tier performance on coding benchmarks with major advances in reasoning and agentic tasks at lower inference cost than Western frontier models.
  *Status: Released*

- **NVIDIA Ising** (NVIDIA)
  The world's first family of open-source AI models purpose-built to accelerate quantum computing. Targets quantum error correction and processor calibration — up to 2.5× faster and 3× more accurate error-correction decoding vs. traditional approaches.
  *Status: Released (open-source)*

- **Zhipu AI GLM-4.7 / GLM-5.1** (Zhipu AI / Z.ai)
  GLM-4.7 is trained entirely on Huawei Ascend silicon and claims a 1.2% hallucination rate — the lowest reported by any frontier lab — at $0.11 per million input tokens. GLM-5.1 is an open-weights coding-focused model that landed at roughly the same agentic engineering ceiling as Western frontier models.
  *Status: Released*

- **Mistral 128B Flagship** (Mistral AI)
  Mistral's latest release includes a new 128B parameter flagship model, async cloud-based coding sessions, and an agentic Work mode integrated into Le Chat.
  *Status: Released*

- **Anthropic Claude Opus 4.7 / Claude Mythos** (Anthropic)
  Claude Opus 4.7 confirmed incoming. A model internally codenamed "Mythos" is in limited internal testing, suggesting another major release on the near horizon. Anthropic shipped four major Claude updates in roughly 50 days during early 2026.
  *Status: Announced / Internal testing*
