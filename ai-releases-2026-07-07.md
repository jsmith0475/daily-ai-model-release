# AI Model Releases - July 7, 2026

## Top Updates

- **gpt-realtime-2.1 & gpt-realtime-2.1-mini — OpenAI**
  New Realtime API models delivering at least 25% lower p95 latency for voice and multimodal experiences, with improved noise handling, language recognition, stronger tool use, and better instruction following. Both models target real-time voice application developers.
  *Status: Released*

- **GPT-5.6 Sol — OpenAI (on Cerebras)**
  Next-generation model announced June 26 and launching in July on Cerebras hardware, delivering up to 750 tokens per second — a major throughput milestone for inference-at-scale workloads.
  *Status: Launching July 2026*

- **Claude Sonnet 5 — Anthropic**
  The most capable Sonnet model to date, launched June 30 / July 1, 2026, approaching Opus 4.8 performance levels. Priced at $2/M input tokens (introductory rate through August 31) and now the default model for Free and Pro plans.
  *Status: Released / Default model*

- **Claude Fable 5 — Anthropic**
  Anthropic's flagship model returned July 1 after US Commerce Department export controls were lifted — the model had been offline for nearly three weeks. Returns with an updated classifier blocking a previously identified jailbreak technique.
  *Status: Available (with enhanced security)*

- **MAI-Thinking-1 + 6 new MAI models — Microsoft**
  Microsoft launched a family of seven new in-house models under the Microsoft AI (MAI) brand. MAI-Thinking-1 is the flagship reasoning model, matching leading models on software engineering benchmarks and competing with top-weight-class models.
  *Status: Released*

- **Gemini Omni Flash — Google**
  A natively multimodal model now in API public preview, enabling enterprise and developer video workflows with dynamic, custom multimodal pipelines.
  *Status: Public preview (API)*

- **Gemma 4 12B — Google (Open Source)**
  Latest open model designed to run locally with just 16GB of RAM, enabling on-device AI agents. Part of the broader Gemma 4 family released in April 2026.
  *Status: Released / Open source*

- **Grok 4.5 — xAI**
  The next iteration of xAI's Grok series, currently in private beta deployment at SpaceX and Tesla before a broader rollout.
  *Status: Private beta*

- **Mistral open-weight model (unnamed) — Mistral AI**
  Mistral AI teased an upcoming open-weight model described as "very exciting," with early access opening in July 2026. Full release expected this summer.
  *Status: Early access / Announced*

- **Google Gemini 3.5 Pro** (advanced coding model) is expected later in July, with Google reportedly in talks with the White House ahead of the release.
