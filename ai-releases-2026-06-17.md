# AI Model Releases - June 17, 2026

### Top Updates

- **MAI-Thinking-1 (Microsoft)** — Microsoft's AI Superintelligence Team released its first in-house reasoning model as part of a new family of seven models. Positioned to compete with top-tier reasoning models in the enterprise space. Availability: released today.

- **Microsoft Discovery (Microsoft)** — Agentic AI platform built on Azure, now generally available, designed to support the full scientific research workflow. Targets enterprise researchers with end-to-end automation of science pipelines. Availability: GA today.

- **Qwen3.7 Plus (Alibaba/Qwen via Together AI)** — New open-source model released today via Together AI. Part of the ongoing Qwen3 family expansion with stronger reasoning and instruction-following capabilities. Availability: released today.

- **MiniMax M3 (MiniMax)** — New multimodal model released today. Details on parameter count and benchmarks pending, but positioned as a competitive open offering. Availability: released today.

- **Gemini 3.5 Flash (Google)** — Achieves an Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index score of 55, surpassing Claude Sonnet 4.6 (52) and Grok 4.3 (53). Optimized for speed and multimodal tasks with large context windows. Availability: released (recent).

- **OpenAI Real-Time Audio Models (OpenAI)** — Three new models targeting conversational agents, real-time translation, and transcription use cases. Builds on OpenAI's voice/audio API infrastructure. Availability: released.

- **OpenAI Deployment Simulation (OpenAI)** — Announced June 16: a new evaluation method that replays past production conversations through a candidate model before release, improving safety and regression testing. Not a model itself but a significant release infrastructure update.

- **NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 Content Safety (NVIDIA)** — Customizable multimodal safety model aimed at global enterprise AI deployments. Supports policy customization across regions and content types. Availability: released.

- **Claude Opus 4.8 (Anthropic)** — Holds the #1 position on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index with a score of 61.4 (released May 27, 2026). Remains the top-ranked model as of today's date.

- **Gemini Robotics for Atlas (Google DeepMind + Boston Dynamics)** — Joint announcement on integrating Gemini Robotics models with Boston Dynamics' Atlas humanoid robot platform, targeting physical AI and manipulation tasks. Availability: announced/early development.
