# AI Model Releases - 2026-06-24

## Top Updates

- **Claude Fable 5 & Claude Mythos 5 — Anthropic**
  Launched June 9, representing a significant capability tier above Claude Opus 4.8. Access was subsequently disabled globally on June 12 after the US government issued an export-control directive citing national security concerns — Anthropic could not filter foreign nationals from domestic users in real time.
  - Status: Disabled globally (was: Released)

- **MAI Model Family (7 models) — Microsoft**
  Microsoft unveiled seven in-house AI models at Microsoft Build 2026 under the MAI (Microsoft AI) banner, marking a major push to reduce reliance on OpenAI:
  - **MAI-Thinking-1**: Flagship reasoning model, matches leading models on software engineering benchmarks and advanced math reasoning.
  - **MAI-Code-1-Flash**: Inference-efficient agentic coding model with 5B active parameters; comparable to Haiku but cheaper. Microsoft's first proprietary in-house coding model.
  - **MAI-Transcribe-1.5**: Claimed state-of-the-art transcription accuracy.
  - Status: Released

- **GPT-5.5 family — OpenAI**
  OpenAI's GPT-5.5 variants (GPT-5.5 Pro, GPT-5.5 Instant, GPT-5.5-Cyber) have been rolling out through June 2026. GPT-5.5-Cyber was updated alongside the "Patch the Planet" open-source bug-fix initiative with Trail of Bits.
  - Status: Released (rolling out)

- **MiniMax M3 — MiniMax**
  The first open-weight frontier model combining software-engineering-level code capabilities with a 1-million-token context window and native multimodal computer use. Positioned as a serious open-source competitor to closed frontier models.
  - Key specs: 1M token context, native multimodal, open-weight
  - Status: Released

- **Gemini 2.5 Flash (default) / Gemini 3.5 Pro (upcoming) — Google**
  Gemini 2.5 Flash is now the default model powering all Google Gemini consumer products (Gemini app, Gemini in Search, Gemini in Workspace, Android assistant). Runs at 284 tokens/sec with a 1M token context window. Gemini 3.5 Pro was confirmed at Google I/O for a June 2026 release window.
  - Key specs: 1M token context, 284 tokens/sec
  - Status: Gemini 2.5 Flash — Released; Gemini 3.5 Pro — Announced/Imminent

- **NVIDIA RTX Spark Superchip — NVIDIA**
  Announced at Computex 2026 (June 1), NVIDIA's entry into the consumer laptop chip market. Jensen Huang declared NVIDIA will "reinvent the PC" alongside Microsoft with this new superchip.
  - Status: Announced

- **EUROPA Consortium AI Model — European Commission**
  The European Commission selected the EUROPA Consortium (led by Italian firm Domyn) as winner of its Frontier AI Grand Challenge on June 19. Backed by a dedicated 6,000-chip NVIDIA Blackwell cluster and up to 2.5% of EuroHPC supercomputing capacity.
  - Status: Announced / In development

- **Microsoft + Mayo Clinic Healthcare AI — Microsoft / Mayo Clinic**
  Partnership announced to co-create a frontier AI model specifically for healthcare applications.
  - Status: Announced
