# AI Model Releases - April 22, 2026

> Briefing covering the past 24–48 hours and most significant April 2026 launches.

---

## Top Updates

- **Claude Opus 4.7 — Anthropic** (April 16)
  The most consequential model release of the month. Replaces Opus 4.6 as the default Opus model across all Claude products, the Anthropic API, Amazon Bedrock, Google Cloud Vertex AI, and Microsoft Foundry. Delivers elite coding and agentic performance at a significantly reduced price compared to its predecessor.
  _Status: Released_

- **Grok 4.3 Beta — xAI** (April 17)
  xAI's latest beta iteration of Grok 4, continuing its rapid cadence of model updates.
  _Status: Beta_

- **Gemini 3.1 Ultra — Google DeepMind** (mid-April)
  Flagship of the Gemini 3.1 suite emphasizing native multimodal reasoning and real-time processing across text, image, audio, and video — no transcription intermediaries. Features a 2-million-token context window. Scored 94.3% on GPQA Diamond. Currently tied with GPT-5.4 Pro at the top of the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index (57 pts).
  _Key specs: 2M token context window | 94.3% GPQA Diamond_
  _Status: Released_

- **OpenAI GPT-5.4 Pro / "Thinking" — OpenAI** (April 2026)
  The GPT-5.4 series introduces a "Thinking" variant with enhanced test-time compute. The Thinking variant scored 75.0% on OSWorld-Verified, officially surpassing human-level performance on desktop task benchmarks. Tied at the top of the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index alongside Gemini 3.1 Ultra.
  _Key specs: 75.0% OSWorld-Verified (human-level desktop tasks)_
  _Status: Released_

- **Meta Llama 4 Scout & Maverick — Meta** (April 5)
  First Llama models with Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) architecture and native multimodal training from pretraining. Scout features 17B active parameters and a 10-million-token context window — the largest context window of any open-weight model.
  _Key specs: 17B active params (Scout) | 10M token context window | Open-weight MoE_
  _Status: Released (open-weight)_

- **Meta Muse Spark — Meta** (April 8)
  Meta's first-ever proprietary flagship LLM, built under Chief AI Officer Alexandr Wang's Superintelligence Labs. Competitive on multimodal perception, reasoning, health, and agentic tasks at significantly reduced compute cost. Meta has stated it hopes to open-source future versions.
  _Status: Released (proprietary)_

- **Google Gemma 4 — Google** (April 2)
  Four open-source variants from 2.3B to 31B parameters, all natively multimodal. The 31B Dense variant ranks #3 globally on Arena AI among open models, with a 20× improvement in competitive coding vs. Gemini 3.
  _Key specs: 2.3B–31B params | Apache 2.0 license | Natively multimodal_
  _Status: Released (open-source)_

- **NVIDIA Ising — NVIDIA** (April 2026)
  World's first family of open-source AI models purpose-built to accelerate quantum computing. Delivers up to 2.5× faster and 3× more accurate error-correction decoding vs. traditional approaches.
  _Status: Released (open-source)_

- **Claude Mythos — Anthropic** (April 7, restricted)
  Anthropic's most capable model ever built. Scored 93.9% on SWE-bench Verified and 94.6% on GPQA Diamond, and independently identified thousands of zero-day vulnerabilities. Judged too dangerous for public release; access restricted to ~50 organizations under Project Glasswing.
  _Key specs: 93.9% SWE-bench Verified | 94.6% GPQA Diamond_
  _Status: Not publicly released — restricted access only_
