# AI Model Releases - June 28, 2026

## Top Updates

- **GPT-5.6 Sol — OpenAI**
  Previewed on June 26, GPT-5.6 Sol is OpenAI's next-generation flagship model, positioned above GPT-5.5. No public benchmark details yet; developer preview access is rolling out. OpenAI also unveiled an LLM-optimized inference chip co-developed with Broadcom on June 24, aiming to reduce inference costs at scale.
  *Status: Preview*

- **MAI-Thinking-1 + 6 additional MAI models — Microsoft**
  Announced at Microsoft Build 2026 (June 2–3), MAI-Thinking-1 is Microsoft's flagship in-house reasoning model — a 1T-total-parameter / 35B-active MoE architecture trained from scratch on 33T tokens. Matches leading models on software engineering benchmarks and advanced math. The MAI family also includes MAI-Code-1-Flash (code generation) and MAI Transcribe-1.5 (claimed best-in-class transcription). Signals Microsoft's push to reduce dependence on OpenAI.
  *Status: Released / GA*

- **Gemini 3.5 Pro — Google DeepMind**
  Featuring a 2-million-token context window and a "Deep Think" extended-reasoning mode. Confirmed at Google I/O (late May 2026); full GA expected in July 2026. Gemini 3.5 Flash is already GA (since May 19) and the default model in the Gemini app and Google Search AI Mode ($1.50 / $9.00 per million input/output tokens).
  *Status: Announced / Flash GA, Pro coming July*

- **Grok 4.3 — xAI**
  Continuation of the Grok 4.x series rollout through June 2026. Multi-agent variants available. xAI has been pushing iterative updates roughly every 2–3 weeks in this cycle.
  *Status: Rolling out*

- **Gemini-powered Siri + Claude on iPhone — Apple**
  Announced at WWDC 2026. The rebuilt Siri runs on a custom 1.2-trillion-parameter Gemini model licensed from Google (~$1B/year deal). Claude is now available as a first-party AI option on iPhone for the first time, integrated via Apple Intelligence.
  *Status: Announced (iOS availability TBD)*

- **Claude Fable 5 & Claude Mythos 5 — Anthropic**
  Launched June 9, 2026, representing a new capability tier above Claude Opus 4.8. On June 12, the US government issued an export-control directive restricting access to these models for foreign nationals, citing national security concerns. Domestic access remains available.
  *Status: Released (restricted export)*

- **SubQ 1M-Preview — Subquadratic**
  Long-context specialist model with a 1-million-token context window. Targeting enterprise document analysis and research workflows.
  *Status: Preview*

- **ZAYA1-8B — Zyphra**
  Open-source 8B-parameter model, part of the trend of efficient open models delivering strong performance at reduced compute cost.
  *Status: Released / Open Source*
