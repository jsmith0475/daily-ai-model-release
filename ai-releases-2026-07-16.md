# AI Model Releases - July 16, 2026

## Top Updates

- **Inkling — Thinking Machines Lab (Open Weight)**
  The debut model from Mira Murati's startup, released July 15, 2026. A natively multimodal mixture-of-experts system that reasons across text, image, audio, and video in a single pass, designed to balance cost and performance rather than chase raw benchmark records. Unlike OpenAI or Anthropic flagships, it is open-weight — developers can download and modify it directly.
  *Specs: 975B total parameters (MoE), ~41B active per query; trained on 45T tokens across text, image, audio, and video*
  *Status: Released / Open weight*

- **GPT-5.6 Series (Sol, Terra, Luna) — OpenAI**
  OpenAI's next frontier lineup, going broadly public this week after an announcement on July 8. The three-tier family spans from high-end agentic reasoning down to cheap high-volume work. Sol is OpenAI's most capable agentic model yet, able to split tasks across subagents for long autonomous runs. Terra matches GPT-5.5 quality at roughly half the cost. Luna targets fast, low-cost, high-volume workloads.
  *Specs: Sol — $5.00/1M input, $30.00/1M output; Terra — ~half Sol cost; Luna — budget tier*
  *Status: Public release (Sol was in preview last week)*

- **Claude Sonnet 5 — Anthropic**
  Launched June 30, 2026. The most agentic Sonnet yet, able to plan, use browsers and terminals, and run autonomously at a capability level that previously required larger models. Optimized for tool use, coding, and knowledge work. Introductory pricing runs through August 31.
  *Specs: 1M token context window, 128K max output tokens; $2/1M input · $10/1M output (through Aug 31, then $3/$15)*
  *Status: Available (API: `claude-sonnet-5`)*

- **Grok 4.5 — xAI**
  Released in early July 2026. xAI claims stronger coding and knowledge-work results with reduced token consumption compared to Grok 4.1. Positions as a cost-efficient alternative for developer and agentic workflows.
  *Status: Available*

- **Muse Spark 1.1 — Meta**
  Released July 9, 2026. Meta's agent-focused release, leaning heavily into computer use and autonomous task execution with a 1 million token context window — matching Claude Sonnet 5's context capacity.
  *Specs: 1M token context window*
  *Status: Available*

- **Fable 5 / Mythos 5 (National Security Restrictions Lifted) — Anthropic**
  The U.S. government lifted prior national security restrictions on Anthropic's most capable models, Fable 5 and Mythos 5, making them accessible to a broader set of government and commercial partners. No new model release, but a significant change in availability.
  *Status: Expanded access*

- **Claude Corps Fellowship — Anthropic**
  A paid 12-month fellowship program announced this week to train AI professionals embedded within nonprofit organizations. Not a model release, but a notable workforce development initiative.
  *Status: Announced*

## Notable Ecosystem News

- **Google Africa Applied AI Lab** launched this week in Accra, Ghana, offering African researchers and entrepreneurs early access to Google AI technologies and direct technical guidance.
- **OpenAI drug discovery spin-out:** Researcher Miles Wang is leaving OpenAI to found an AI drug discovery startup, reportedly raising ~$200M at a $2B valuation. Highlights the ongoing migration of frontier AI talent into applied science verticals.
- July 2026 is one of the most active stretches for AI model launches on record — trackers estimate new models are arriving at an average of once every three days.
