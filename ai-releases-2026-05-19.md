# AI Model Releases - 2026-05-19

## Top Updates

- **Qwen3 Coder Next — Alibaba**
  Next-generation code-specialist LLM with long-context support designed for multi-file project workflows. Released May 18 via Alibaba Cloud and Hugging Face.
  _Status: Released_

- **MiniMax M2.5 Highspeed & M2.7 Highspeed — MiniMax AI**
  Two new latency-optimized LLMs targeting real-time chat and customer-support use cases; M2.7 variant adds improved reasoning on top of the speed gains. Both released May 18 via the MiniMax API.
  _Status: Released_

- **GPT-5.5 Instant — OpenAI**
  Lightweight flagship now serving as the default ChatGPT model across web and mobile. Scores 81.2 on AIME 2025 math benchmark. Rolled out May 5.
  _Status: Released (now ChatGPT default)_

- **Grok 4.3 — xAI**
  Improved reasoning and coding model with reduced hallucinations and native X-platform data integration. Broader rollout began May 6 for X Premium subscribers.
  _Status: Released_

- **SubQ 1M-Preview — Subquadratic**
  First commercial subquadratic-architecture LLM, supporting a 12M-token context window at roughly one-fifth the compute cost of transformer-based equivalents. Preview access opened May 5 to select research teams.
  _Specs: 12M-token context; ~0.2× transformer cost at scale_
  _Status: Preview (research access)_

- **ZAYA1-8B — Zyphra**
  Apache 2.0-licensed 8B-parameter mixture-of-experts model optimized for AMD silicon, achieving frontier-adjacent reasoning at just 760M active parameters. Released open-source May 6–7.
  _Specs: 8B total / 760M active params; Apache 2.0_
  _Status: Released (open-source)_

- **Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite — Google**
  Entry-level Gemini model designed for high-volume gateway workloads; hit production APIs May 8. Ahead of Google I/O 2026 (May 19–20), Google is also expected to unveil a new major Gemini version and the "Omni" conversational video-generation system.
  _Status: Released; further Gemini announcements expected at Google I/O today_

- **NVIDIA Ising — NVIDIA**
  First open-source AI model family purpose-built to accelerate quantum computing — targeting error correction (up to 2.5× faster decoding) and processor calibration (up to 3× more accurate). Early adopters include Harvard, Fermilab, and Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory.
  _Status: Announced / open-source_
