# AI Model Releases - May 10, 2026

## Top Updates

- **GPT-5.5 Instant — OpenAI**
  The new default model for ChatGPT, with personalized memory that can reference past conversations, uploaded files, and Gmail. Scores 81.2 on AIME 2025 (vs 65.4 for its predecessor) and 76 on MMMU-Pro multimodal reasoning (vs 69.2). Available to Plus and Pro users on web; mobile rollout coming soon. — *Released*

- **GPT-5.5-Cyber — OpenAI**
  A security-focused variant of GPT-5.5, rolled out in limited preview to vetted cybersecurity teams. Designed to assist with threat analysis and security operations. — *Limited Preview*

- **Gemini 3.1 Ultra — Google**
  Google's flagship 2026 model featuring a 2-million-token context window with native multimodal understanding across text, image, audio, and video (no transcription intermediaries). — *Released*

- **Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite — Google**
  Efficiency-focused model delivering 2.5× faster response times and 45% faster output generation compared to earlier Gemini versions. Priced at $0.25 per million input tokens. — *Released*

- **Grok 4.3 — xAI**
  xAI's latest reasoning model, now available on Oracle OCI. Strong performance on logic, math, coding, and multi-step analysis tasks, with a 1-million-token context window. — *Released*

- **Chinese Open-Weights Coding Models — Z.ai, MiniMax, Moonshot, DeepSeek**
  Four Chinese labs released open-weights coding-focused models within a 12-day window: **GLM-5.1** (Z.ai), **MiniMax M2.7**, **Kimi K2.6** (Moonshot), and **DeepSeek V4**. All reach a similar capability ceiling on agentic engineering benchmarks at meaningfully lower inference cost than Western frontier models. — *Open-weights Released*

- **Ising — NVIDIA**
  The world's first family of open-source AI models purpose-built to accelerate quantum computing workloads. — *Announced/Released*

- **Anthropic Enterprise AI Services Company — Anthropic / Blackstone / Goldman Sachs**
  Anthropic, Blackstone, Hellman & Friedman, and Goldman Sachs announced formation of a new enterprise AI services company to bring Claude into mid-sized company operations across sectors. — *Announced*

## On the Horizon

- **Google I/O 2026** is scheduled for **May 19–20**, with registration now open. Further model announcements expected.
