# AI Model Releases - July 15, 2026

> Daily briefing covering the latest AI model releases, announcements, and launches from the past 24–48 hours and the broader week.

---

## Top Updates

- **GPT-5.6 Sol / Terra / Luna — OpenAI**
  OpenAI's next-generation GPT-5.6 family launched publicly on July 9 and became ChatGPT's default model. Sol targets high-end reasoning, coding, biology, and cybersecurity; Terra delivers GPT-5.5-level performance at ~half the cost; Luna is optimized for speed and high-volume, lower-cost workloads.
  _Pricing:_ Sol $5/$30 per 1M tokens (in/out) · Terra $2.50/$15 · Luna $1/$6 | Status: **Generally available**

- **GPT-5.6 in Microsoft 365 Copilot — OpenAI × Microsoft**
  On July 14, Microsoft announced GPT-5.6 is now the preferred model powering Microsoft 365 Copilot across enterprise customers.
  Status: **Live**

- **Claude Sonnet 5 — Anthropic**
  Released June 30 and now the default model for Free and Pro plans, Claude Sonnet 5 is Anthropic's most agentic model yet — capable of autonomously using browsers and terminals with near-Opus 4.8 performance at significantly lower cost.
  _Pricing:_ $2/$10 per 1M tokens (introductory until Aug 31, 2026; then $3/$15) | Status: **Generally available**

- **Grok 4.5 — xAI (SpaceXAI)**
  Announced July 8 by Elon Musk and launched July 9, Grok 4.5 is positioned as an Opus-class model that is faster, more token-efficient, and lower cost. Available to SuperGrok Heavy and Premium+ subscribers, plus xAI API.
  Status: **Generally available**

- **Meta Muse Spark 1.1 — Meta**
  Released July 9, Muse Spark 1.1 is Meta's agentic model with a 1M-token context window. Highlights include computer use across desktop, browser, and mobile, and parallel subagent delegation. Ships with Meta's first-ever paid developer API in public preview ($20 free credits, US-only at launch).
  Status: **Public preview (US)**

- **Meta Muse Image — Meta**
  Launched July 7, Muse Image is Meta's most advanced image generation model. Understands complex prompts, can blend photos, and generates functional QR codes.
  Status: **Released**

- **Innovative Eyewear × Claude — Anthropic integration**
  Innovative Eyewear integrated Claude AI across its entire Lucyd smart eyewear lineup. Users can choose between Claude and ChatGPT and switch mid-conversation without losing context.
  Status: **Available**

- **NAVER HyperCLOVA X + NVIDIA DSX — NAVER × NVIDIA**
  NAVER and NVIDIA announced an expanded sovereign AI infrastructure partnership. NAVER's next-generation HyperCLOVA X models and an AI Agent Platform are targeting a Korean launch in H2 2026, running on NVIDIA's DSX platform.
  Status: **Announced, H2 2026 launch**

## Industry Pulse

- The AI industry is averaging a new significant model release roughly **every 3 days**.
- Reasoning models (o1-class, DeepSeek-R1 descendants) continue trading speed for accuracy and are now mainstream across frontier labs.
- Multimodal capabilities (text + image + computer use) are becoming a baseline expectation, not a differentiator.
- Microsoft has launched **Microsoft Frontier Company**, a new $2.5B operating business with 6,000 experts focused on enterprise AI deployment success (announced July 2).
