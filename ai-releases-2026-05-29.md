## AI Model Releases - May 29, 2026

### Top Updates

- **GPT-5.5 Instant — OpenAI**
  The latest in OpenAI's rapid-release cadence (shipped ~6 weeks after GPT-5.4), GPT-5.5 Instant became the default ChatGPT model on May 5, 2026. Focuses on speed and low-latency responses while maintaining GPT-5-class capability.
  *Status: Generally available*

- **GPT-5.3-Codex — OpenAI**
  OpenAI's most capable agentic coding model, merging the Codex and GPT-5 training stacks. Approximately 25% faster than its predecessor and sets new highs on coding and agentic benchmarks.
  *Status: Released*

- **Gemini 3.5 Flash — Google**
  Announced at Google I/O 2026 (May 19–20), Gemini 3.5 Flash delivers frontier-level intelligence at Flash-series speed — 4× faster output tokens per second than comparable flagships. Surpasses Gemini 3.1 Pro on coding, agentic, and multimodal benchmarks.
  *Status: Available via Gemini API*

- **Gemini Omni — Google**
  A new model series combining Gemini's reasoning with multimodal generation. Gemini Omni Flash accepts image, audio, video, and text input and outputs video grounded in real-world knowledge.
  *Status: Announced at Google I/O 2026*

- **NVIDIA Ising — NVIDIA**
  Open-source family of AI models purpose-built to accelerate quantum computing. Delivers up to 2.5× faster and 3× more accurate quantum error-correction decoding compared to traditional approaches.
  *Status: Open-source release*

- **Mistral 128B Flagship — Mistral AI**
  Mistral's new 128B-parameter flagship model ships with async cloud coding sessions and a new Work agentic mode integrated into Le Chat. Targets enterprise coding and long-horizon agentic tasks.
  *Status: Released*

- **SubQ Architecture Model — SubQ**
  A non-transformer architecture startup that raised $29M seed funding and launched its first model on May 5 with a native 12 million token context window. Positions itself as a post-transformer alternative for long-context workloads.
  *Specs: 12M token context window | Status: Released*

- **ZAYA1-8B — Zyphra**
  Mixture-of-Experts model released May 6, 2026, activating only ~760M parameters per token during inference despite an 8B total parameter count. Designed for efficient edge and cloud deployment.
  *Specs: 8B params, ~760M active per token | Status: Released*

- **Anthropic "Dreaming" Agent Technique — Anthropic**
  Anthropic unveiled a new AI agent improvement technique called "dreaming" — autonomous systems review prior behavior between sessions, identify patterns, and update their own performance without human intervention.
  *Status: Research announcement*
