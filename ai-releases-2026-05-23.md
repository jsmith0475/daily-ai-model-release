# AI Model Releases - May 23, 2026

## Top Updates

- **Gemini 3.5 Flash — Google**
  Launched at Google I/O 2026 (May 19–21), Gemini 3.5 Flash is the first model in Google's new action-oriented series, combining frontier intelligence with agentic capabilities. It outperforms Gemini 3.1 Pro on challenging coding, agentic, and multimodal benchmarks at 4× the speed of comparable frontier models.
  *Key specs: Rivals large flagship models in intelligence; Flash-class latency and cost*
  *Status: Generally available — Gemini API, Google AI Studio, Android Studio*

- **Gemini Omni — Google**
  A new multimodal model series unveiled at Google I/O 2026 that pairs Gemini's reasoning with generation. Accepts image, audio, video, and text as input and can output video grounded in real-world knowledge. Available in Google Flow to Google AI subscribers globally.
  *Status: Available (Google AI subscribers)*

- **Google Flow Agent — Google**
  Announced alongside I/O 2026, Flow Agent is an upgraded agentic platform that can orchestrate multi-step tasks rather than single-prompt execution, now integrated with the Gemini 3.5 model family.
  *Status: Available*

- **Qwen3 Coder Next — Alibaba**
  Released May 19, 2026, this is a next-generation code-specialist variant of the Qwen3 family, tuned specifically for software engineering workflows and repository-scale reasoning tasks.
  *Status: Released*

- **MiniMax M2.5 Highspeed & M2.7 Highspeed — MiniMax**
  Released May 18, 2026. Two high-throughput, latency-optimized models designed for production deployments requiring speed and scale. M2.7 targets higher-capability workloads while M2.5 prioritizes throughput.
  *Status: Released*

- **SubQ — SubQ (Startup)**
  First commercial subquadratic large language model, featuring a 12 million token context window. Represents an architectural shift away from the standard transformer quadratic attention bottleneck, enabling significantly longer context at lower compute cost.
  *Key specs: 12M context window; subquadratic attention architecture*
  *Status: Released (commercial)*

- **ZAYA1-8B — Zyphra**
  An open-source Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) reasoning model with 8B total parameters and approximately 760M active parameters per token. Notably trained end-to-end on AMD Instinct hardware, demonstrating serious non-NVIDIA training viability.
  *Key specs: 8B total / ~760M active (MoE); AMD Instinct training*
  *Status: Released / Open source*

- **Ising — NVIDIA**
  NVIDIA's first family of open-source AI models purpose-built to accelerate quantum computing research and applications. Named after the Ising model from statistical mechanics.
  *Status: Released / Open source*
