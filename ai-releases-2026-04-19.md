# AI Model Releases - April 19, 2026

### Top Updates

- **Claude Opus 4.7 — Anthropic**
  Released April 16, this is Anthropic's newest flagship model targeting complex reasoning and long-running agentic workflows. Lands in the same historic week as GPT-6 and Meta's LlamaCon releases.
  *Status: Released*

- **Gemini 3.1 Ultra — Google DeepMind**
  Features a 2-million token context window with native multimodal reasoning across text, image, audio, and video — trained from the ground up for cross-modal tasks.
  *Status: Released*

- **Claude Mythos Preview — Anthropic**
  Originally planned for public release, Claude Mythos (reportedly 10 trillion parameters, optimized for cybersecurity and coding) is now rolling out exclusively to a consortium of 40+ tech and cybersecurity companies.
  *Status: Limited preview*

- **Meta Llama 4 Maverick & Scout — Meta**
  Released April 5 as open-weight Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) models. Both are available for download and target on-device and research use cases.
  *Status: Released (open-weight)*

- **NVIDIA Ising — NVIDIA**
  World's first family of open-source AI models purpose-built to accelerate quantum computing error correction — up to 2.5× faster and 3× more accurate than traditional decoders.
  *Status: Released (open-source)*

- **Gemma 4 — Google DeepMind**
  Compact multimodal model family (2B, 4B, 26B, 31B params) co-optimized with NVIDIA for local and edge deployment; supports reasoning, coding, vision, and audio tasks.
  *Status: Released*

- **GPT-5.4 — OpenAI**
  Released in Standard, Thinking, and Pro variants. Sits at the top of the OpenAI lineup as the company surpasses 900M weekly ChatGPT users and $2B/month in revenue.
  *Status: Released*

- **Meta Muse Spark — Meta**
  Meta's first flagship LLM out of its new Superintelligence Labs (led by Chief AI Officer Alexandr Wang). Competitive on multimodal perception, reasoning, health, and agentic tasks at lower compute cost.
  *Status: Announced / Early access*
