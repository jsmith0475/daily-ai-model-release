# AI Model Releases - April 15, 2026

## Top Updates

- **Claude Mythos — Anthropic**
  Anthropic's most capable model ever built, confirmed April 7, 2026. Designed for extreme reasoning and agentic tasks, it surfaced publicly via a data leak on March 26 before Anthropic officially acknowledged it. Due to significant cybersecurity risks (including exposing vulnerabilities in banking infrastructure), it will **not** receive a general public release. Access is restricted to a handpicked consortium of 40+ technology and cybersecurity companies under Project Glasswing.
  *Status: Restricted / Partner access only*

- **GPT-5.4 Pro — OpenAI**
  OpenAI's most capable general-purpose model, shipping in three variants: Standard, Thinking (reasoning-first), and Pro (maximum capability). Tied with Gemini 3.1 Ultra at 57 points on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index — the highest publicly accessible score. Features a 1.05 million token context window.
  *Status: Available (tiered access)*

- **Gemini 3.1 Ultra / Gemini 3.0 — Google**
  Gemini 3.0 debuted as a flagship "thinking" model that claimed the top spot on major benchmarks. Gemini 3.1 Ultra is now tied with GPT-5.4 Pro at the top of the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index. Google also released **Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite**, an efficiency-focused variant with 2.5x faster responses and 45% faster output, priced at $0.25 per million input tokens.
  *Status: Available*

- **Gemma 4 — Google (Open Source)**
  Released April 2, 2026 under Apache 2.0. Comes in four variants ranging from 2.3B to 31B parameters, all natively multimodal. The 31B Dense model ranks #3 globally on Arena AI among open models. Competitive coding performance (Codeforces ELO) jumped from 110 in Gemma 3 to 2150 — a ~20x improvement. Smaller variants are designed to run on smartphones.
  *Status: Released / Open source (Apache 2.0)*

- **Llama 4 Scout & Maverick — Meta (Open Weight)**
  Meta's latest open-weight releases using a Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) architecture. Scout targets efficient on-device and edge deployment while Maverick is optimized for higher-capability tasks. Both are available under Meta's open license.
  *Status: Released / Open weight*

- **Kimi K2 Thinking — Moonshot AI (Open)**
  An open reasoning model from Moonshot AI setting new records on reasoning benchmarks. Designed with a "thinking" inference mode for multi-step problem solving. Released in April 2026.
  *Status: Released / Open*

- **Grok 4.1 — xAI**
  xAI's latest release combining high emotional intelligence with strong logical reasoning, topping the LM Arena leaderboard as of mid-April 2026. Positioned as a leading conversational and agentic model.
  *Status: Available*

- **FLUX 2 — Black Forest Labs (Open Weight)**
  A high-performance open-weight image generation model, the successor to the popular FLUX.1 series. Released in April 2026 with improved photorealism and prompt adherence.
  *Status: Released / Open weight*

- **NVIDIA Physical AI Models + Jetson T4000**
  NVIDIA released new open models and frameworks for physical AI (robotics). The Jetson T4000 module (Blackwell architecture) is now available, delivering 4x greater energy efficiency and AI compute compared to its predecessor — targeted at next-generation robot deployments by global partners.
  *Status: Available*
