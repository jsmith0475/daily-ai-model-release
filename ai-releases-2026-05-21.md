# AI Model Releases - May 21, 2026

> Daily briefing covering the latest AI model launches, announcements, and updates from the past 24–48 hours.

---

## Top Updates

- **Qwen3 Coder Next — Alibaba** *(Released May 18, 2026)*
  Next-generation code-specialist LLM tuned for software engineering and repository-scale reasoning, featuring enhanced long-context support. Targets competitive performance on complex multi-file coding tasks.
  *Availability: Released*

- **MiniMax M2.5 Highspeed & M2.7 Highspeed — MiniMax** *(Released May 18, 2026)*
  A pair of latency-optimized, high-throughput LLMs designed for real-time chat, customer support, and production inference pipelines. Focus is on speed-to-token at scale.
  *Availability: Released*

- **Gemini 3.5 Flash — Google** *(Announced at Google I/O 2026)*
  Now the default model in Google's AI Mode in Search globally. Also available in the Gemini app and developer/enterprise APIs. Paired with the Antigravity agentic platform for building mini-apps (wedding planning, fitness tracking, etc.).
  *Availability: Generally Available*

- **Gemini 3.1 Ultra — Google** *(Recent)*
  2-million token context window with native multimodal support across text, image, audio, and video. Represents Google's top-of-frontier offering alongside the 3.5 Flash series.
  *Specs: 2M token context | Multimodal | Availability: Released*

- **NVIDIA Ising — NVIDIA** *(Announced May 2026)*
  World's first family of open-source AI models purpose-built to accelerate quantum computing — targeting quantum error correction and processor calibration. Claims 2.5× faster and 3× more accurate error-correction decoding vs. traditional approaches.
  *Availability: Open-source release*

- **Mistral 128B — Mistral AI** *(Released May 3, 2026)*
  Mistral's 128-billion parameter flagship model with async cloud coding sessions and a new "Work" agentic mode in Le Chat. Targets enterprise and developer workloads requiring large-scale reasoning.
  *Specs: 128B parameters | Availability: Released*

- **SubQ Subquadratic LLM — SubQ** *(Recent)*
  First commercial subquadratic-attention LLM, supporting a 12-million token context window. Challenges the transformer attention bottleneck at extreme context lengths.
  *Specs: 12M token context | Subquadratic architecture | Availability: Released*

- **Zyphra 8B MoE — Zyphra** *(Recent)*
  8-billion parameter Mixture-of-Experts model notably trained entirely on AMD hardware, signaling growing GPU diversity in LLM training pipelines.
  *Specs: 8B params | MoE architecture | Trained on AMD | Availability: Released*
