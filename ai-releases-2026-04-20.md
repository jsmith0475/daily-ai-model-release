# AI Model Releases - April 20, 2026

### Top Updates

- **GPT-5.4 (OpenAI)** — Launches in three tiers: Standard, Thinking (reasoning-first), and Pro (maximum capability). Features a 1.05 million token context window with 33% fewer factual claim errors compared to GPT-5.2. *Status: Released*

- **Gemini 3.1 Ultra (Google DeepMind)** — Flagship multimodal model with a 2-million token context window supporting native text, image, audio, and video reasoning without transcription intermediaries. Scores 94.3% on GPQA Diamond and leads 13 of 16 major benchmarks, tying GPT-5.4 Pro on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at roughly one-third the API cost. Ships with a sandboxed Code Execution tool for in-conversation coding. Also released: **Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite** (2.5× faster, 45% faster output generation). *Status: Released*

- **Gemma 4 (Google / NVIDIA)** — Open-weight family (E2B, E4B, 26B, 31B parameter variants) under Apache 2.0, co-optimized by NVIDIA for local and edge deployment. Targets on-device reasoning, coding, vision, and audio tasks at frontier-level performance. *Status: Released*

- **Claude Mythos 5 (Anthropic)** — 10-trillion parameter model confirmed via data leak on April 7. Not publicly released due to cybersecurity risks; available only to select partners under Project Glasswing. Anthropic also announced a mid-sized companion model, **Capabara**, targeting coding and cybersecurity use cases. *Status: Limited / Partner Access Only*

- **Grok 4.20 (xAI)** — Introduces a 4-agent collaborative architecture: Grok (coordinator), Harper (research), Benjamin (logic/math), and Lucas (contrarian analysis), running in parallel with cross-verified outputs. *Status: Beta 2*

- **Meta Llama 4 Maverick (Meta)** — Open-weight model at 400B parameters with a 10 million token context window, currently the largest open-weight context offering available. *Status: Released*

- **Muse Spark (Meta)** — Meta's first flagship LLM under Chief AI Officer Alexandr Wang's newly formed Superintelligence Labs. Competitive on multimodal perception, reasoning, health, and agentic tasks at reduced compute cost vs. Llama 4 mid-size variants. *Status: Released*

- **Ising (NVIDIA)** — World's first family of open-source AI models purpose-built to accelerate quantum computing, specifically targeting quantum error correction and processor calibration. *Status: Released*
