# AI Model Releases - April 20, 2026

## Top Updates

- **Grok 4.20 — xAI**
  Upgraded from Grok 4.1 with a novel 4-agent parallel architecture: Grok (coordinator), Harper (research), Benjamin (logic/math), and Lucas (contrarian analysis) cross-verify outputs before responding. Enhanced real-time web access and improved factual accuracy round out the release.
  *Status: Available (Beta 2)*

- **Meta Muse Spark — Meta AI (Superintelligence Labs)**
  Meta's first flagship LLM built under Chief AI Officer Alexandr Wang's newly formed Superintelligence Labs. Delivers competitive multimodal perception, reasoning, health, and agentic task performance at significantly lower compute cost than the older Llama 4 Maverick (400B). Represents Meta's pivot toward a more centralized frontier model strategy backed by $115–135B in 2026 capex.
  *Status: Released*

- **NVIDIA Ising — NVIDIA (Open Source)**
  The world's first family of open-source AI models purpose-built to accelerate quantum computing. Achieves up to 2.5x faster and 3x more accurate quantum error-correction decoding compared to traditional approaches — targeting practical quantum advantage on near-term hardware.
  *Status: Released / Open source*

- **GLM-5.1 — Zhipu AI (Open Source)**
  Released under the MIT license, GLM-5.1 surpasses GPT-5.4 on SWE-bench Pro coding benchmarks, making it the top open-source model for agentic software engineering tasks. Offers frontier-level intelligence at a fraction of the hardware requirements of larger MoE models.
  *Status: Released / Open source (MIT)*

- **Claude Capybara — Anthropic**
  Anthropic's new mid-sized model released alongside the restricted Claude Mythos. Targets practical, everyday agentic workloads with a balance of capability and efficiency. Available via Anthropic API; positioned below Mythos but above Claude Sonnet 4.6 in the capability hierarchy.
  *Status: Available (API)*

- **Apple Siri (AI-Powered Redesign) — Apple**
  Apple officially announced a fully reimagined AI-native Siri debuting in 2026, powered by a partnership with Google using Gemini models running on Apple's Private Cloud Compute infrastructure. Marks Apple's most significant Siri overhaul since its 2011 launch.
  *Status: Announced / Launching 2026*

- **Huawei 950PR AI Chip — Huawei**
  Huawei's new inference-optimized AI chip targeting domestic Chinese cloud deployments. ByteDance and Alibaba have placed large orders, signaling a significant shift in China's AI hardware supply chain away from NVIDIA-restricted GPUs.
  *Status: Available (China market)*

- **Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite — Google**
  Efficiency-focused variant of Gemini 3.1, delivering 2.5x faster response times and 45% faster output generation versus prior Flash variants. Priced at $0.25 per million input tokens, making it the most cost-effective frontier-class model available.
  *Status: Available*
