# AI Model Releases - July 9, 2026

### Top Updates

- **OpenAI GPT-5.6 (Sol, Terra, Luna)** — OpenAI publicly launches all three GPT-5.6 variants today after a government-requested delay over national security concerns. Sol is the new flagship, Terra is mid-range for everyday work, and Luna is a fast, low-cost option. The Trump administration cleared the release after additional testing and review meetings with OpenAI. *(Released – July 9, 2026)*

- **OpenAI GPT-Live-1 & GPT-Live-1 mini** — New generation of voice models that can listen and speak simultaneously, enabling real-time conversational interaction without turn-taking delays. Both variants are rolling out globally to ChatGPT users today. *(Released – July 9, 2026)*

- **Anthropic Claude Sonnet 5** — Anthropic's latest model brings improved long-run coding, tool use, and debugging capabilities at a reduced price point. Priced at $2/M input tokens and $10/M output tokens (rising to $3/$15 after August 31, 2026). *(Released – June 30, 2026)*

- **Google Gemini "Nano Banana 2 Light"** — Fast image-generation model producing outputs in approximately 4 seconds. Part of Google's early-July generative media model update. *(Released – early July 2026)*

- **Google "Gemini OVNI Flash"** — Short video generation model (~10 seconds of video) priced at approximately $0.10 per second. Also part of Google's July generative media release wave. *(Released – early July 2026)*

- **NVIDIA Nemotron-Labs-TwoTower** — Open-weight diffusion language model that generates text in parallel, delivering 2.42× higher throughput while maintaining 98.7% of baseline quality. Designed for high-efficiency inference at scale. *(Announced – July 2026)*

- **Google TabFM** — Zero-shot foundation model for tabular data supporting both classification and regression tasks without task-specific training or manual feature engineering. *(Released – July 2026)*

- **Microsoft Frontier Company** — Microsoft launched a dedicated AI deployment operating business backed by a $2.5B investment and 6,000 industry and engineering experts, focused on enterprise AI deployment using Microsoft's existing AI tooling. *(Announced – July 2, 2026)*
