# AI Model Releases - July 8, 2026

### Top Updates

- **Claude Sonnet 5 (Anthropic)** — Frontier coding and agentic model launched June 30, 2026. Scores 63.2% on SWE-bench Pro, outperforming Claude Opus 4.8 on Terminal-Bench 2.1, with particular improvements in complex agentic tasks and instruction-following. Priced at $2/$10 per million tokens (input/output) through August 31, 2026. *Status: Generally available.*

- **Claude Fable 5 (Anthropic)** — Returned July 1, 2026. As of today (July 8), all Fable 5 access requires usage credits for every subscriber tier. *Status: Available (usage-credit gated).*

- **GPT-5.6 Sol / Terra / Luna (OpenAI)** — Three-tier model family launched June 26, 2026. Sol ($5/$30/M tokens) achieves 91.9% on Terminal-Bench 2.1 Sol Ultra; Terra ($2.50/$15/M) is mid-tier; Luna ($1/$6/M) is the new budget tier. Currently restricted to government-vetted and trusted partners; broad release expected this week per July 7 reporting. *Status: Limited preview, wide release imminent.*

- **Gemini 3.5 Pro (Google)** — Frontier model still in limited Vertex AI enterprise preview as of this week. No GA date, published benchmarks, or confirmed pricing available yet. *Status: Enterprise preview only.*

- **Gemini Omni Flash (Google)** — Natively multimodal model brought to APIs in public preview. Targets enterprise and developer use cases requiring low-latency multimodal inference. *Status: Public preview.*

- **Gemma 4 12B (Google)** — Open-weight model designed to run locally on consumer hardware using ~16 GB of memory. Enables on-device agentic AI without cloud dependency. *Status: Released (open weights).*

- **Runway Gen-4 Turbo (Runway)** — Faster inference variant of Runway's Gen-4 video generation model, reducing generation time by ~60% with comparable output quality. *Status: Released.*

- **Grok 4.5 (xAI)** — Next-generation Grok model in private beta, currently accessible only to SpaceX and Tesla internal teams. No public benchmarks or release timeline announced. *Status: Private beta.*
