# AI Model Releases - June 29, 2026

## Top Updates

- **Claude Fable 5 & Claude Mythos 5 — Anthropic**
  Anthropic's most powerful models to date, launched June 9, 2026, representing a significant capability tier above Claude Opus 4.8. Claude Opus 4.8 has since claimed the top spot on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence leaderboard. On June 12, the US government issued an export-control directive citing national security concerns, barring access by foreign nationals.
  *Status: Available with restrictions (US only per export controls)*

- **GPT-5.6 Sol, Terra & Luna — OpenAI**
  OpenAI's next generation of flagship models, previewed June 26, 2026. Sol is the lead model in the family; Terra and Luna are specialized variants. At the US government's request, access is currently restricted to "trusted partners" only, with general availability planned in coming weeks. GPT-4.5 was simultaneously retired from ChatGPT.
  *Status: Trusted partner access only*

- **Gemini 3.5 Pro — Google DeepMind**
  Google's latest frontier model features a massive 2-million-token context window and a specialized "Deep Think" cognitive mode for extended reasoning tasks. The ultra-fast companion model Gemini 3.5 Flash went GA on May 19 at Google I/O 2026 ($1.50/$9.00 per million tokens).
  *Status: Gemini 3.5 Flash GA; Gemini 3.5 Pro releasing in June*

- **MAI Model Family (7 models) — Microsoft**
  Microsoft unveiled seven in-house AI models at Microsoft Build 2026. Flagship highlights include **MAI-Thinking-1**, a 1T-parameter total / 35B-active MoE reasoning model trained from scratch on 33T tokens (no distillation), matching top models on software engineering and math benchmarks; and **MAI-Code-1-Flash**, an inference-efficient agentic coding model purpose-built for GitHub Copilot and VS Code. Microsoft and Mayo Clinic are also co-developing a frontier healthcare AI model for clinical reasoning.
  *Status: Announced / Releasing*

- **Grok Imagine Video 1.5 — xAI**
  xAI's latest video generation model delivers nearly 2x faster generation speeds, adds native audio output, and claims the #1 position on video generation leaderboards.
  *Status: Released*

- **MiniMax 3 — MiniMax (Shanghai)**
  A multimodal powerhouse from Chinese AI lab MiniMax, achieving a strong Elo rating on competitive benchmarks. Priced aggressively at $0.53 per 1M tokens, making it one of the most cost-efficient frontier multimodal models available.
  *Status: Released / Available via API*

- **Qwen 3.7 Max — Alibaba / Qwen Team**
  Alibaba's latest open model matches Western flagship performance on major benchmarks while priced at $3.75 per 1M tokens — dramatically undercutting comparable Western models and continuing the trend of Chinese models compressing the cost of intelligence.
  *Status: Released / Open*

- **Devin Desktop (formerly Windsurf) — Cognition**
  Cognition rebranded the Windsurf IDE into Devin Desktop, a multi-agent command center with Agent Client Protocol (ACP) support, consolidating its IDE acquisition into the Devin agent brand.
  *Status: Released*
