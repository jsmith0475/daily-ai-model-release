# AI Model Releases - May 18, 2026

### Top Updates

- **Google Gemini 3.1 Ultra** — Google's most significant model release of 2026, featuring a native 2-million token context window across text, image, audio, and video without transcription intermediaries. Considered a major multimodal leap over the 3.0 series. *(Released)*

- **OpenAI GPT-5.5 Instant** — Became the new default ChatGPT model on May 5, 2026, replacing the previous default across Free, Plus, and Pro tiers. Scores 81.2 on AIME 2025 math and 76 on MMMU-Pro multimodal reasoning; supports memory integration across past conversations, uploaded files, and Gmail. *(Released / Live)*

- **DeepSeek V4 Flash & V4 Pro** — Chinese AI startup DeepSeek released two new open-source models via Alibaba and Tencent infrastructure. Both claim top-tier performance on coding benchmarks and significant advancements in reasoning and agentic tasks. *(Released / Open-source)*

- **Meta Muse Spark** — Meta's first flagship LLM developed under Chief AI Officer Alexandr Wang's newly formed Superintelligence Labs. Targets competitive performance on multimodal perception, reasoning, health, and agentic tasks at a fraction of the compute cost of its older Llama 4 mid-size variants. *(Announced / Released)*

- **Mistral 128B Flagship** — Mistral launched a new 128-billion parameter model supporting async cloud coding sessions and a new "Work" agentic mode inside its Le Chat product. *(Released)*

- **NVIDIA Ising** — NVIDIA unveiled Ising, the world's first family of open-source AI models purpose-built to accelerate quantum computing, targeting quantum error correction and processor calibration. Delivers up to 2.5× faster and 3× more accurate error-correction decoding vs. traditional approaches. Adopters include Harvard, Fermi National Accelerator Laboratory, and Lawrence Berkeley National Lab. *(Released / Open-source)*

- **SubQ Subquadratic LLM** — SubQ shipped the first commercial subquadratic large language model, featuring a 12-million token context window — a potential architectural inflection point for long-context inference efficiency. *(Released)*

- **Anthropic "Dreaming" Agent Technique** — Anthropic introduced a research preview of a new AI agent technique called "dreaming," enabling autonomous systems to review prior behavior, identify patterns, and self-improve between sessions. Targets long-running workflows in coding, finance, and law. *(Research Preview)*
