# AI Model Releases - July 4, 2026

### Top Updates

- **Claude Sonnet 5 — Anthropic**
  The most capable Sonnet to date and now the default model for all Free and Pro Claude users as of July 1. Performs close to the flagship Opus 4.8 on many tasks, with notable improvements in multi-step tool use, structured output reliability, and memory management across long agentic sessions. Introductory pricing through August 31 makes it cheaper than Sonnet 4.6.
  *Status: Released (June 30, 2026)*

- **MAI-Thinking-1 & MAI-Code-1-Flash — Microsoft AI**
  Part of a family of seven new in-house MAI models. MAI-Thinking-1 is Microsoft's flagship reasoning model, matching leading frontier models on software engineering benchmarks. MAI-Code-1-Flash is an inference-efficient agentic coding model purpose-built for GitHub Copilot and VS Code.
  *Status: Announced / Released*

- **Gemini 3.1 Flash Image & Gemini 3 Pro Image — Google**
  Two new image-generation models released June 30. Flash Image targets cost-efficient workflows at $0.50/M input tokens and $3.00/M output tokens; Pro Image is the high-quality tier at $2.00/M input and $12.00/M output. Both are available via API.
  *Status: Released (June 30, 2026)*

- **Gemma 4 12B — Google DeepMind**
  Google's latest open model designed to run locally on consumer hardware with 16 GB of RAM. Targets smart on-device AI agents and developer experimentation without cloud dependency.
  *Context window / params: 12B parameters; local deployment*
  *Status: Released*

- **o4-mini (July checkpoint) — OpenAI**
  An updated build of OpenAI's compact reasoning model with improved performance on AIME-class multi-step math and logic problems and a reduced hallucination rate on complex reasoning chains. No pricing change.
  *Status: Released (July 2026)*

- **GPT-5.6 Sol — OpenAI**
  Next-generation model previewed June 26, 2026. Details remain limited but it is positioned as a step above GPT-5 on flagship benchmarks.
  *Status: Preview*

- **Llama 4 (low-resource language fine-tune) — Meta**
  An updated fine-tune of Llama 4 expanding usable multilingual coverage to dozens of low-resource languages where AI assistance has historically been limited.
  *Status: Released*

- **Fable 5 — Anthropic**
  Returned to global availability on July 1, 2026.
  *Status: Released*
