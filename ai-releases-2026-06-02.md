# AI Model Releases - 2026-06-02

### Top Updates

- **Gemini 3.5 Flash — Google**
  First model in Google's latest series combining frontier intelligence with autonomous action. Generally available via Google AI Studio, Gemini API, and Android Studio. Outperforms Gemini 3.1 Pro on challenging coding and agentic benchmarks while maintaining Flash-series speed.
  *Status: Released (GA)*

- **Gemini 3.5 Pro — Google**
  Follows Flash as the flagship model in the Gemini 3.5 family; expected to deliver deeper reasoning and stronger multimodal capabilities than Gemini 3.1 Pro.
  *Status: Rolling out June 2026*

- **GPT-5.3-Codex — OpenAI**
  OpenAI's most capable agentic coding model, merging the Codex and GPT-5 training stacks. Runs ~25% faster than predecessors and sets new highs on coding benchmarks while supporting complex multi-step code generation and execution.
  *Status: Released*

- **GPT-5.4 — OpenAI**
  Combines GPT-5.3-Codex's industry-leading coding with broader reasoning and agentic workflow capabilities. Designed for professional tasks spanning tools, APIs, and autonomous pipelines.
  *Status: Released*

- **Claude Opus 4.8 — Anthropic**
  Incremental upgrade over Opus 4.7 with across-the-board benchmark improvements. Achieves 84% on Online-Mind2Web for computer use, strengthening its position as a top-tier agentic model. (Alongside this release, Anthropic announced a $65B Series H round valuing the company at ~$965B.)
  *Status: Released*

- **Gemma 4 — Google DeepMind**
  Open-weight model optimized for advanced reasoning and agentic workflows. Continues Google's line of openly accessible Gemma models, targeting developers building autonomous agents.
  *Status: Released (open weights)*

- **Muse Spark — Meta Superintelligence Labs**
  Meta's first release from its Superintelligence Labs; natively multimodal reasoning model with built-in tool-use, visual chain-of-thought, and multi-agent orchestration support. First model in the Muse family.
  *Status: Released (announced April 8, 2026)*
