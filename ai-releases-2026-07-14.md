# AI Model Releases - July 14, 2026

> Daily briefing covering new model launches, major updates, and AI infrastructure announcements from the past 24–48 hours.

---

## Top Updates

- **OpenAI GPT-5.6 Sol / Terra / Luna** (OpenAI) — The GPT-5.6 family moved from a government-gated preview to broad public availability on July 9. Sol is the flagship tier, tuned for biology, chemistry, and cybersecurity; Terra matches GPT-5.5 performance at roughly half the cost; Luna prioritizes speed and affordability. Pricing: Sol at $5/$30, Terra at $2.50/$15, Luna at $1/$6 per million input/output tokens. **Status: Generally available.**

- **OpenAI GPT-Live-1 & GPT-Live-1 mini** (OpenAI) — Full-duplex voice models released alongside GPT-5.6. Both can speak and listen simultaneously, enabling natural interruption and real-time translation. GPT-Live-1 mini targets low-latency, cost-sensitive use cases. **Status: Released.**

- **OpenAI ChatGPT Work** (OpenAI) — A multi-hour autonomous agent combining Codex and a built-in browser. Natively integrates with Google Drive, Slack, and Salesforce. Designed for long-horizon enterprise tasks rather than single-turn queries. **Status: Released.**

- **xAI Grok 4.5** (xAI) — Released July 8, 2026. Listed across multiple model trackers as a new frontier release; specific benchmark details are limited, but it is positioned as a step up from Grok 4 in reasoning and multimodal capabilities. **Status: Released.**

- **Anthropic Claude Sonnet 5** (Anthropic) — Launched July 1. Described as Anthropic's most agentic model to date; autonomously uses tools including browsers and terminals. Delivers near-Opus 4.8 performance at meaningfully lower cost. Best-rated model in the current roundup for instruction-following and writing style. **Status: Released.**

- **Meta (first paid model)** (Meta) — Meta shipped its first commercially priced model alongside the July 9 frontier wave. Specific model name and pricing tiers have not yet been widely reported, but it marks a significant shift from Meta's historically open-weight-only strategy. **Status: Released.**

- **Cognition sub-$2 coding model** (Cognition AI) — A new coding-focused model priced under $2 per million tokens, released the week of July 9 as part of the broader frontier model wave. Positioned to compete on cost efficiency for software development workflows. **Status: Released.**

- **Muse Spark 1.1** (Muse AI) — Features a 1,000,000-token context window with a strong emphasis on agent tasks and computer use. Positioned for long-document analysis and multi-step automated workflows. **Status: Released.**

## Infrastructure & Ecosystem

- **OpenAI Jalapeño Chip** — OpenAI announced its custom inference chip, codenamed Jalapeño, on July 1. Intended to reduce dependence on third-party GPU suppliers and lower per-token inference costs at scale.
- **Blackstone AI Data Centers** — Blackstone committed $30 billion to AI data center buildout in Japan, reflecting accelerating demand for inference compute globally.
- **Qualcomm acquires Modular** — Qualcomm completed a $4 billion acquisition of AI startup Modular, signaling a push to consolidate AI compiler and runtime technology for edge/on-device inference.
