# AI Model Releases - June 12, 2026

## Top Updates

- **Microsoft MAI Family (Microsoft)**
  Seven new in-house MAI models launched at the Build developer conference in San Francisco. Highlights include **MAI-Thinking-1**, a flagship reasoning model that matches leading models on software engineering benchmarks and demonstrates advanced mathematical reasoning (currently in private preview via Microsoft Foundry), and **MAI-Code-1-Flash**, Microsoft's first code-generation model that converts written descriptions into application source code. Goal: reduce developer reliance on OpenAI and lower inference costs.
  *Status: MAI-Code-1-Flash released; MAI-Thinking-1 private preview*

- **Gemini 3.5 Flash / Gemini 3.5 Pro (Google)**
  Gemini 3.5 Flash launched at Google I/O (late May) with an Intelligence Index score of 55 — surpassing Claude Sonnet 4.6 (52) and Grok 4.3 (53) — at 284 tokens/second output speed. Gemini 3.5 Pro is confirmed for June 2026 release, targeting complex enterprise and agentic workloads with deeper reasoning and longer context handling.
  *Status: 3.5 Flash released; 3.5 Pro imminent*

- **Gemini 3.1 Ultra (Google)**
  Google's most significant model launch of the year features a **2-million token context window** and native multimodal processing across text, image, audio, and video without transcription intermediaries.
  *Status: Released*

- **Apple WWDC — Gemini-Powered Siri & Claude on iPhone (Apple / Google / Anthropic)**
  At WWDC, Apple unveiled a new Siri running on a custom **1.2-trillion-parameter Gemini model** licensed from Google (~$1B/year). Apple also introduced a multi-AI Extensions system that makes **Claude** available as a first-party AI option on iPhone for the first time, alongside iOS 27 Beta 1.
  *Status: Announced at WWDC; iOS 27 Beta 1 available*

- **Meta Muse Spark (Meta)**
  Meta's first flagship LLM built under Chief AI Officer Alexandr Wang's newly formed Superintelligence Labs. Delivers competitive performance on multimodal perception, reasoning, health, and agentic benchmarks at significantly lower compute cost than the Llama 4 mid-size variant it succeeds.
  *Status: Released*

- **Claude Mythos Preview / Project Glasswing (Anthropic)**
  Anthropic launched Project Glasswing, a controlled program giving select enterprise partners — including AWS, Apple, Cisco, Google, JPMorgan Chase, and Microsoft — early access to **Claude Mythos Preview**, Anthropic's unreleased frontier model, specifically to identify and remediate critical software vulnerabilities.
  *Status: Limited preview (select partners only)*

- **GPT-5.4 / GPT-5.3-Codex (OpenAI)**
  OpenAI released GPT-5.3-Codex, merging the Codex and GPT-5 training stacks for enhanced code generation and reasoning (~25% faster than its predecessor). GPT-5.4 follows with industry-leading coding capabilities and improved performance across agentic tool use and professional tasks.
  *Status: Released*

- **Intel Rackscale AI Infrastructure — Computex 2026 (Intel)**
  Intel announced new rackscale AI infrastructure at Computex 2026 combining **Intel Xeon processors** and **SambaNova SN-50 Reconfigurable Dataflow Units (RDUs)**, targeting customers scaling inference and agentic workloads at the data-center level.
  *Status: Announced at Computex 2026*
