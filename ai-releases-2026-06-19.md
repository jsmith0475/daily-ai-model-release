## AI Model Releases - June 19, 2026

### Top Updates

- **Claude Opus 4.8 — Anthropic**
  Anthropic's latest flagship model has dethroned GPT-5.5 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Leaderboard, cementing it as the top-ranked LLM as of mid-June 2026. Claude Opus 4.8 is now available inside Microsoft's Foundry catalog (alongside ~11,000 other models) and is expected broadly by end of June. *Status: Released*

- **MiniMax 3 — MiniMax (Shanghai)**
  A multimodal powerhouse from Chinese AI lab MiniMax, achieving a 1,528 Elo rating — matching Western flagship models. Priced aggressively at $0.53 per 1M tokens, it signals the effective closure of the US–China AI performance gap. *Status: Released*

- **Gemini 3.5 Flash — Google DeepMind**
  Launched at Google I/O (late May 2026), Gemini 3.5 Flash posted an Intelligence Index score of 55, surpassing Claude Sonnet 4.6 (52) and Grok 4.3 (53). Output speed is 284 tokens/second. *Status: Released*

- **Gemini 3.5 Pro — Google DeepMind**
  Confirmed at Google I/O as arriving "next month" (June 2026); expected before the end of June. Anticipated to push above Gemini 3.5 Flash on benchmark scores. *Status: Imminent / Announced*

- **Gemini-Powered Siri + Multi-AI Extensions — Apple**
  Announced at WWDC on June 8, 2026: the new Siri runs on a custom 1.2-trillion-parameter Gemini model licensed from Google ($~1B/year). iOS 27 introduces a multi-AI Extensions system, making Claude the first non-Apple/non-Google AI natively selectable on iPhones. Features a standalone Siri app, Dynamic Island integration (iPhone 16+), personal context access, and deep cross-app actions. *Status: Beta (iOS 27 Beta 1)*

- **Real-Time Audio Models (×3) — OpenAI**
  OpenAI released three specialized real-time audio models targeting conversational agents, live translation, and transcription. Alongside these, a self-serve Ads Manager inside ChatGPT was launched, opening advertiser tooling to broader audiences. *Status: Released*

- **Nemotron 3.5 Content Safety — NVIDIA**
  A customizable multimodal safety model built for global enterprise AI deployments, handling safety checks across text, image, and audio modalities with support for regional regulatory requirements. *Status: Released*

- **Claude Mythos Preview (Project Glasswing) — Anthropic**
  A specialized defensive-cybersecurity model made available to ~50 partner organizations including AWS, Apple, Google, Microsoft, NVIDIA, CrowdStrike, and JPMorgan Chase. Launched April 7, 2026; continuing to expand in June. *Status: Limited Partner Preview*

- **Qwen 3.7 Max — Alibaba**
  Alibaba's latest Qwen flagship is benchmarking on par with top Western models, contributing to the broader trend of Chinese labs eliminating the performance gap at significantly lower inference costs. *Status: Released*

---

### Key Trends This Week
- **US–China parity**: MiniMax 3 and Qwen 3.7 Max match Western flagships at a fraction of the cost.
- **Device-level AI**: Apple's Gemini-powered Siri and the multi-AI Extensions framework push LLMs into OS-level UX.
- **Specialization**: Safety models (NVIDIA), cybersecurity models (Anthropic), and audio models (OpenAI) reflect a move from general to task-specific releases.
- **Infrastructure consolidation**: Microsoft Foundry now catalogs 11,000+ models including Opus 4.8 and Gemini 3.5 Pro.
