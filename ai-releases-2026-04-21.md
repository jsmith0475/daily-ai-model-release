## AI Model Releases - April 21, 2026

### Top Updates

- **Claude Opus 4.7 — Anthropic**
  Anthropic's newest flagship model, released April 16, targeting complex reasoning and long-running agent workflows. Lands in the same historic week as GPT-6 and Meta's LlamaCon releases. Available via API and Claude.ai.

- **Claude Mythos 5 — Anthropic**
  Previewed to select partners; features a reported 10 trillion parameters with a focus on advanced cybersecurity and coding use cases. Availability: limited preview.

- **GPT-6 — OpenAI**
  OpenAI's next-generation flagship LLM launched this week, competing at the frontier alongside Gemini 3.1 and Claude Opus 4.7. Full specs not yet public. Available via ChatGPT and API.

- **Gemini 3.1 Ultra — Google DeepMind**
  Leads 13 of 16 major benchmarks and scores 94.3% on GPQA Diamond. Features a 2-million token context window with native multimodal reasoning (text, image, audio, video) and an integrated sandboxed Code Execution tool. Available via Google AI Studio and Vertex AI.

- **Gemma 4 — Google DeepMind**
  Four open-weight variants released under Apache 2.0, delivering frontier-level performance in an open-source package. Available on Hugging Face and Google AI platforms.

- **Llama 4 Maverick — Meta**
  400B-parameter open-weight model with a 10-million token context window, now the top open-weight option for long-context tasks. Released open source.

- **Grok 4.1 — xAI**
  Combines high EQ with strong logic reasoning, currently topping the LM Arena leaderboard. Available via xAI API and Grok.com.

- **Ising (Quantum AI Models) — NVIDIA**
  World's first family of open-source AI models for quantum computing. Delivers quantum error-correction decoding up to 2.5× faster and 3× more accurate than traditional approaches, plus best-in-class quantum processor calibration. Released open source.
