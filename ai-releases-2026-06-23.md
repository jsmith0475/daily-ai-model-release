# AI Model Releases - June 23, 2026

> Covering the past 24–48 hours and most significant recent launches. No major frontier model dropped on June 23 itself; most action this week was consolidation around earlier June releases.

---

## Top Updates

- **Microsoft MAI-Thinking-1 (Microsoft AI)** — Microsoft's headline reasoning model from the MAI family (7 models total), a 1T-parameter MoE architecture with 35B active parameters, trained from scratch on 33T tokens without distillation. Matches leading models on software-engineering benchmarks and advanced math. *Released at Microsoft Build 2026 (June 2–3). Available via Azure AI.*

- **Microsoft MAI-Transcribe-1.5 (Microsoft AI)** — Speech-to-text model claimed to be the best transcription model in the world with state-of-the-art accuracy across languages. Part of the same seven-model MAI family launch. *Available now.*

- **OpenAI GPT-5.3-Codex (OpenAI)** — Unified coding + general-purpose model merging the Codex and GPT-5 training stacks. Delivers best-in-class code generation, reasoning, and agentic workflow orchestration; 25% faster than its predecessor with new benchmark highs. *Released; available via API and ChatGPT.*

- **OpenAI GPT-5.5-Cyber (OpenAI)** — Security-focused variant of the GPT-5.5 line, launched alongside the "Patch the Planet" open-source bug-fixing initiative in partnership with Trail of Bits. *Available now.*

- **Google Gemini 3.5 Pro / Flash (Google DeepMind)** — Gemini 3.5 Pro introduces a 2-million-token context window and a specialized "Deep Think" cognitive reasoning mode for complex tasks. Gemini 3.5 Flash is the paired ultra-fast, low-cost variant. *Generally available.*

- **Claude Fable 5 & Claude Mythos 5 (Anthropic)** — New capability tier above Claude Opus 4.8, representing Anthropic's most capable models to date. Fable 5 targets multimodal reasoning and long-context tasks; Mythos 5 is positioned as the frontier research model. *Released June 9, 2026; available via Claude.ai and API.*

- **xAI Grok Imagine Video 1.5 (xAI)** — Video generation model claiming the #1 position on video generation leaderboards, featuring ~2× faster generation than its predecessor and native audio integration. *Released; available via Grok platform.*

- **Apple Intelligence / Gemini-Powered Siri (Apple)** — Fully rebuilt Siri leveraging Gemini under the hood, with a multi-AI Extensions framework that makes Claude available as an on-device AI option for the first time on iPhone (iOS 27). Adds onscreen awareness and deep personal-context understanding. *Announced at WWDC June 8; iOS 27 Beta 1 released same day.*
