# AI Model Releases - May 12, 2026

### Top Updates

- **Gemini 3.1 Ultra — Google**
  The most significant Google model release of the year, featuring a native 2-million token context window that works across text, image, audio, and video without any transcription intermediaries. Released to general availability this month.

- **Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite — Google**
  Efficiency-focused sibling to Ultra delivering 2.5× faster response times and 45% faster output generation. Priced at $0.25 per million input tokens, making it Google's most cost-effective multimodal option. Available now.

- **Grok 4.1 + Grok Imagine 1.0 — xAI**
  Grok 4.1 tops the LM Arena leaderboard by combining high EQ with strong logical reasoning. Alongside it, xAI launched Grok Imagine 1.0, a fully functional video generation platform. Note: SpaceX has acquired xAI, folding Grok into a broader corporate ecosystem. Released.

- **Claude Opus 4.5 — Anthropic**
  Delivers elite coding and agentic task performance at a significantly reduced price compared to prior Opus models. Anthropic also introduced a new "dreaming" agent technique (research preview) that lets autonomous systems review prior behavior between sessions to improve future performance. Available now.

- **Meta Muse Spark — Meta**
  Meta's first flagship LLM built under Chief AI Officer Alexandr Wang's newly formed Superintelligence Labs. Targets competitive multimodal perception, reasoning, health, and agentic tasks at a fraction of the compute cost of Llama 4 mid-size variants. Announced / early availability.

- **Mistral 128B — Mistral AI**
  128-billion-parameter flagship model featuring async cloud coding sessions and a new Work agentic mode integrated into Le Chat. Released.

- **Chinese Open-Weights Coding Models — Multiple Labs**
  Four Chinese labs released open-weights coding models within a 12-day window: **GLM-5.1** (Z.ai), **MiniMax M2.7**, **Kimi K2.6** (Moonshot AI), and **DeepSeek V4** — all converging at roughly the same capability ceiling on agentic engineering benchmarks. Open-source / released.

- **NVIDIA Ising — NVIDIA**
  The world's first family of open-source AI models purpose-built to accelerate quantum computing, targeting quantum error correction and processor calibration — two of the biggest barriers to practical quantum systems. Announced.
