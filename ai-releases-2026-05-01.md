# AI Model Releases - May 1, 2026

## Top Updates

- **GPT-5.5 — OpenAI**
  Incremental but significant step beyond GPT-5.2, with improved coding, computer-use, and deep-research capabilities. Includes stricter cybersecurity safeguards and classifiers. Rolling out to Plus, Pro, Business, and Enterprise ChatGPT users; also available in limited preview on Amazon Bedrock.
  _Status: Rolling out (GA for paid tiers)_

- **Gemini 3.1 Ultra — Google**
  Features a native 2-million-token context window and true multimodal reasoning across text, image, audio, and video from training — not bolted-on post-processing. Announced at Google Cloud Next 2026.
  _Status: Released / announced_

- **Muse Spark — Meta**
  Meta's first flagship LLM built under Chief AI Officer Alexandr Wang's newly formed Superintelligence Labs. Targets competitive performance on multimodal perception, reasoning, health, and agentic tasks at a fraction of comparable compute cost.
  _Status: Announced_

- **DeepSeek V4 (Flash & Pro) — DeepSeek**
  Successor to the V3 models that shook Silicon Valley in early 2025. Introduces a "Hybrid Attention Architecture" for dramatically improved long-context memory, with top-tier coding benchmark scores and advances in reasoning and agentic tasks. Unveiled April 24, 2026.
  _Status: Released_

- **Ising — NVIDIA**
  World's first family of open-source AI models purpose-built for quantum computing — specifically targeting quantum error correction and processor calibration. Represents NVIDIA's push into quantum-AI convergence.
  _Status: Released (open-source)_

- **Moonshot AI FlashKDA — Moonshot AI**
  High-performance implementation of Kimi Delta Attention (KDA), plugging directly into the flash-linear-attention ecosystem. Targets efficient long-context inference for developers building on Kimi-family models.
  _Status: Released (open-source)_

- **Microsoft Windows AI Agents — Microsoft**
  AI agents integrated into the Windows 11 taskbar, accessible via click or "@" mention. Includes Microsoft 365 Researcher and other agents for in-OS agentic workflows. Rolling out this week.
  _Status: Rolling out (Windows 11)_
