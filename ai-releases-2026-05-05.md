# AI Model Releases - May 5, 2026

### Top Updates

- **OpenAI GPT-5.5 Cyber AI** — A specialized cybersecurity-focused model released today (May 5, 2026). Designed for offensive and defensive cyber tasks; Sam Altman announced restricted distribution to a handpicked group of cyber defenders. The UK AI Security Institute called it "one of the strongest models tested on cyber tasks." Availability: limited/restricted rollout.

- **DeepSeek V4 (Z.ai / DeepSeek)** — Released in preview April 24, with both V4-Pro and V4-Flash open-sourced. V4-Pro features **1.6 trillion parameters** and a **1-million-token context window**. Launched with a 75% price cut and OpenAI-compatible API to minimize switching costs. Availability: open-source / API preview.

- **Google Gemini 3.1 Ultra** — Google's flagship multimodal model with a **2-million-token context window**, natively processing text, image, audio, and video. Claims top benchmark positions across reasoning and multimodal tasks. Availability: released.

- **Chinese Open-Weights Coding Cluster** — Four Chinese labs released competitive open-weights coding/agentic models within a 12-day window: **Z.ai GLM-5.1**, **MiniMax M2.7**, **Moonshot Kimi K2.6**, and **DeepSeek V4**. All reached roughly the same capability ceiling as Western frontier models on agentic engineering benchmarks at significantly lower inference cost. Availability: open-source.

- **Black Forest Labs FLUX 2** — High-performance open-weight image generation model, successor to FLUX 1. Delivers improved photorealism and prompt adherence. Availability: open-source / released.

- **xAI Grok Imagine 1.0** — xAI's fully functional video generation platform, extending Grok's capabilities into native video synthesis. Availability: released (via xAI/Grok platform).

- **IBM watsonx Orchestrate (Think 2026)** — Announced at IBM Think 2026 (May 5), the next-generation multi-agent orchestration platform enabling enterprise AI workflows at scale, paired with IBM Confluent for real-time data and IBM Concert for intelligent operations. Availability: announced / enterprise preview.

- **Suno v5** — Major upgrade to Suno's AI music generation platform, delivering studio-grade audio fidelity and more natural-sounding vocals. Availability: released.
