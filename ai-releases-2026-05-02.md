# AI Model Releases - May 2, 2026

## Top Updates

- **GPT-5.5 / OpenAI** — OpenAI's latest and self-described "smartest and most intuitive" model, launched April 23, 2026. Excels at agentic coding, computer use, knowledge work, and scientific research. GPT-5.5 Pro is rolling out to Pro, Business, and Enterprise ChatGPT tiers; also available in preview on Amazon Bedrock. *(Released)*

- **Gemini 3.1 Ultra / Google** — Features a 2-million-token context window and native multimodal reasoning across text, image, audio, and video simultaneously (no transcription intermediaries). Ships with a sandboxed Code Execution tool that lets the model write, run, and test code mid-conversation. *(Released)*

- **Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite / Google** — Lightweight sibling to Gemini 3.1 Ultra priced at $0.25 per million tokens, targeting cost-sensitive deployments. *(Released)*

- **Muse Spark / Meta** — Meta's first flagship large language model, delivering competitive performance on multimodal perception, reasoning, health, and agentic tasks at significantly lower compute cost than Llama 4 mid-size. Announced alongside Meta's 2026 AI capex guidance of $115–135 billion (nearly double 2025). *(Released)*

- **Ising / NVIDIA** — World's first open-source AI model family purpose-built to accelerate quantum computing. Delivers up to 2.5× faster and 3× more accurate quantum error-correction decoding compared to traditional approaches. *(Released)*

- **GLM-4.7 / Zhipu AI** — Frontier model trained entirely on Huawei Ascend silicon (no NVIDIA hardware). Claims a 1.2% hallucination rate — the lowest self-reported figure from any frontier lab to date. *(Released)*

- **DeepSeek V3 (2026) / DeepSeek** — DeepSeek unveiled its newest flagship model on April 24, 2026, roughly one year after its initial market disruption. Full benchmark details are still emerging. *(Released)*

- **Claude Opus 4.7 / Anthropic** — Confirmed upcoming release by Anthropic; a separate internal project code-named "Claude Mythos" is reportedly in limited internal testing. *(Announced / Coming soon)*
