# AI Model Releases - June 15, 2026

### Top Updates

- **Gemini 3.5 Flash — Google**
  Launched at Google I/O 2026, achieving an Intelligence Index score of 55 — surpassing Claude Sonnet 4.6 (52) and Grok 4.3 (53). Optimized for speed at 284 tokens/second. Google also announced Managed Agents in the Gemini API and a broader "agentic Gemini era" push across Google AI Studio.
  *Status: Released*

- **GPT-5.5 (Pro & Instant) — OpenAI**
  OpenAI's latest flagship generalist model, with a Pro variant for premium strategy/writing work and an Instant variant for lower-latency use cases. Continues to lead in general reasoning benchmarks.
  *Status: Released*

- **OpenAI Real-Time Audio Models — OpenAI**
  Three new models targeting conversational AI agents, live translation, and transcription. Designed to compete with specialized voice/audio pipelines.
  *Status: Released*

- **Claude Mythos Preview (Project Glasswing) — Anthropic**
  Released April 7 to ~50 partner organizations (AWS, Apple, Google, Microsoft, NVIDIA, CrowdStrike, JPMorgan Chase, and others) for defensive cybersecurity work only. Represents Anthropic's first restricted-access safety-focused deployment.
  *Status: Limited beta (partner access only)*

- **NVIDIA Nemotron 3 Ultra 550B A55B — NVIDIA**
  Most recent tracked model release (June 4). A 550B-parameter mixture-of-experts architecture with a 55B active parameter footprint. Targets enterprise inference and agentic workloads.
  *Status: Released (June 4)*

- **Gemini-Powered Siri + Multi-AI Extensions — Apple**
  Tim Cook announced iOS 27 Beta 1 with a new Siri running on a custom 1.2-trillion-parameter Gemini model licensed from Google. A new multi-AI Extensions system also allows Claude to serve as an on-device AI option for the first time on iPhone.
  *Status: Beta (iOS 27 Beta 1)*

- **Microsoft Azure AI Foundry Catalog Update — Microsoft**
  Microsoft finalized its 11,000-model Foundry catalog, now including Claude Opus 4.8. Gemini 3.5 Pro and Claude Sonnet 4.8 are expected to be added before end of June.
  *Status: Available (Opus 4.8); others announced*

- **Intel Rackscale AI Infrastructure — Intel**
  Unveiled at Computex 2026 (June 2): new rackscale inference hardware combining Intel Xeon processors with SambaNova SN-50 Reconfigurable Dataflow Units (RDUs), targeting large-scale agentic workloads.
  *Status: Announced*
