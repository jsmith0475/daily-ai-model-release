# AI Model Releases - June 4, 2026

### Top Updates

- **MAI-Code-1-Flash — Microsoft** *(Released June 2, 2026)*
  Microsoft's inaugural coding model, takes written descriptions and generates source code for applications and websites. Announced at Microsoft Build 2026 in San Francisco alongside the repositioning of Windows as a first-class environment for autonomous AI agents.
  *Status: Generally available*

- **MAI-Thinking-1 — Microsoft** *(Announced June 2, 2026)*
  Reasoning-focused model available via Microsoft Foundry (Microsoft's service for integrating models into applications). Currently in private preview; customers can express interest to join early testing.
  *Status: Private preview*

- **Gemini 3.5 Flash — Google** *(Launched at Google I/O 2026)*
  First model in Google's latest series combining frontier intelligence with agentic action capabilities. Generally available via Google Antigravity, the Gemini API, Google AI Studio, and Android Studio. Part of an ongoing Gemini 3.x family momentum into mid-2026.
  *Status: Generally available*

- **GPT-5.3-Codex — OpenAI**
  Merges the Codex and GPT-5 training stacks to deliver best-in-class code generation and reasoning. Approximately 25% faster than prior versions with new benchmark highs on coding tasks. Also available on AWS.
  *Status: Released*

- **GPT-5.4 — OpenAI**
  Brings together reasoning, coding, and agentic workflow advances; incorporates GPT-5.3-Codex capabilities while improving performance across tools, software environments, and professional tasks.
  *Status: Released*

- **Claude Opus 4.8 — Anthropic** *(Released May 28, 2026)*
  Latest frontier model from Anthropic in the Claude 4.x family. Most recent tracked release on the major LLM tracking platforms entering June 2026.
  *Status: Released*

- **Intel Rackscale AI Infrastructure — Intel** *(Announced June 2, 2026, Computex)*
  Not a model, but a significant hardware announcement: Intel unveiled new rackscale AI inference infrastructure combining Intel Xeon processors with SambaNova SN-50 Reconfigurable Dataflow Units (RDUs), aimed at scaling inference and agentic workloads.
  *Status: Announced*

- **Hunyuan HY3 Preview — Tencent · Qwen3.7 Max — Alibaba · Grok Build 0.1 — xAI**
  A wave of additional model releases noted in the past 24–48 hours from Chinese and independent labs. Details on specs and benchmarks are emerging; Qwen3.7 Max and Grok Build 0.1 are both positioned as strong open/semi-open alternatives in the reasoning and coding segments.
  *Status: Released / Preview*
