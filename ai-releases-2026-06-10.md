# AI Model Releases - June 10, 2026

### Top Updates

- **Microsoft MAI Family (Microsoft AI)** — Microsoft launched seven new in-house MAI models today, reducing reliance on OpenAI and targeting lower developer costs. Key releases include **MAI-Thinking-1** (flagship reasoning model competitive on software engineering and math benchmarks), **MAI-Code-1-Flash** (5B active-parameter agentic coding model built for GitHub Copilot and VS Code), **MAI-Image-2.5** (text-to-image and image editing), and **MAI Transcribe-1.5** (claimed best-in-class transcription). Availability: GA / released.

- **Claude Fable 5 (Anthropic)** — Released June 9, 2026. A creative-specialist model optimized for long-form fiction, scripts, and character voices; positioned as a lighter, lower-cost companion to Claude Opus/Sonnet. Aimed at developers building storytelling and entertainment applications. Availability: released.

- **Meta Muse Spark (Meta Superintelligence Labs)** — Meta's first flagship LLM developed under Chief AI Officer Alexandr Wang's newly formed Superintelligence Labs. Delivers competitive performance on multimodal perception, reasoning, healthcare tasks, and agentic workflows at significantly lower compute cost than Llama 4. Availability: announced / limited access.

- **Gemini 3.5 Flash (Google DeepMind)** — Went GA on May 19 at Google I/O 2026. High-speed, low-latency multimodal model optimized for production apps and AI-in-Search. Pricing: $1.50 / $9.00 per million input/output tokens. Availability: GA.

- **Gemini 3.1 Ultra (Google DeepMind)** — Features a 2-million-token context window with native multimodal support across text, image, audio, and video (no transcription intermediaries required). Targets enterprise long-context and cross-modal reasoning workloads. Availability: released.

- **Gemma 4 12B (Google)** — Open-weight 12B-parameter model released ~May 23–24, 2026. Designed for general-purpose coding, reasoning, and tooling with strong fine-tuning support and efficient inference. Availability: open release.

- **Claude Mythos Preview / Project Glasswing (Anthropic)** — Anthropic's controlled early-access initiative granting select enterprise partners (AWS, Apple, Cisco, Google, JPMorgan Chase, Microsoft) preview access to its unreleased frontier model. Focus is on identifying and remediating critical software vulnerabilities. Availability: limited invite-only preview.

- **Microsoft × Mayo Clinic Healthcare Model** — Announced alongside the MAI family: a co-developed frontier AI model targeting clinical reasoning and broad healthcare use cases. No public release date yet. Availability: announced.
