# AI Model Releases - July 5, 2026

## Top Updates

- **Claude Sonnet 5 — Anthropic**
  Launched July 1 as the new default model for all Free and Pro users worldwide, described as the most agentic Sonnet ever built and approaching flagship Opus 4.8 performance. Priced at $2/M input tokens (introductory rate through August 31). A follow-up update in early July improves instruction-following, code generation, multi-step tool use, structured output reliability, and memory management across long sessions.
  *Status: Released*

- **Fable 5 — Anthropic**
  Fully restored to global access as of July 1, 2026 following the lifting of a US government export control order on June 30. Available across Claude.ai, Claude Platform, Claude Code, and Claude Cowork.
  *Status: Released (restored)*

- **MAI-Thinking-1 & Six Additional MAI Models — Microsoft**
  Microsoft AI launched a family of seven new in-house models. MAI-Thinking-1 is the flagship reasoning model — a medium-sized model that matches leading models on software engineering benchmarks and demonstrates advanced mathematical reasoning. Microsoft also announced a collaboration with Mayo Clinic to co-create a frontier AI model for healthcare.
  *Status: Announced / Released*

- **GPT-5.6 Sol — OpenAI**
  Deployed on Cerebras hardware achieving up to 750 tokens per second. Previewed June 26, now in broader availability. OpenAI also shipped an updated July checkpoint for o4-mini improving performance on multi-step math and logic (AIME-class problems) and reducing hallucination rates on complex reasoning chains.
  *Status: Released*

- **Gemini 3.1 Flash Image & Gemini 3 Pro Image — Google**
  Two new image-generation models released June 30. Gemini 3.1 Flash Image is priced at $0.50/M input tokens and $3.00/M output tokens. Gemini 3 Pro Image targets higher quality at $2.00/M input and $12.00/M output.
  *Status: Released*

- **Gemma 4 12B — Google**
  Open model designed to run locally on consumer hardware (16 GB RAM). Features a unified architecture with vision and native voice processing in a single model, aimed at enabling smart AI agents on personal devices.
  *Status: Released*

- **Llama 4 Low-Resource Language Fine-tune — Meta**
  Updated fine-tune of Llama 4 optimized for instruction-following in dozens of low-resource languages, expanding AI assistance to language communities where coverage has historically been limited.
  *Status: Released*

- **Microsoft Frontier Company — Microsoft**
  New operating unit announced July 2, backed by $2.5 billion and approximately 6,000 engineers and industry specialists, focused on embedding AI experts directly with enterprise clients to co-design and deploy AI systems at scale.
  *Status: Announced*
