# AI Model Releases - June 30, 2026

## Top Updates

- **GPT-5.6 Sol — OpenAI**
  OpenAI's latest frontier model, released June 26, with sibling models Terra and Luna also previewed. OpenAI is currently limiting access to "trusted partners" following a U.S. government request, but has stated it believes in broad access and plans wider availability in the coming weeks.
  *Status: Limited / Trusted-partner access only*

- **Claude Fable 5 — Anthropic**
  Anthropic's newest flagship model, released June 9, 2026. Positions as a frontier reasoning and agentic model; also newly available as a first-party AI option inside Apple's iOS 27 through Apple's multi-AI Extensions system, marking the first time Claude is a built-in iPhone assistant option.
  *Status: Available*

- **Fugu Ultra — Sakana AI**
  Sakana AI's latest release, shipped June 22, 2026. Continues the company's focus on biology-inspired, evolutionary AI architectures.
  *Status: Released*

- **Microsoft MAI Model Family (7 models) — Microsoft AI**
  Unveiled at Microsoft Build 2026 (June 2–3) by Satya Nadella. Key models include: **MAI-Thinking-1** (flagship reasoning model, matches leading models on software engineering benchmarks and advanced math), **MAI-Code-1-Flash** (inference-efficient agentic coding model deeply integrated into GitHub Copilot and VS Code), **MAI-Transcribe-1.5** (claimed best transcription accuracy worldwide across 43 languages), **MAI-Image-2.5** (image generation), and **MAI-Voice-2** (speech generation). Microsoft and Mayo Clinic are also co-developing a frontier healthcare AI model.
  *Status: Available (varies by model)*

- **KLING v3.0 Turbo — AtlasCloud**
  Video generation model released June 20, 2026. The latest iteration of the KLING video series with improved generation speed and quality.
  *Status: Released*

- **Grok Imagine Video 1.5 — xAI**
  xAI's updated video generation model, released June 17, 2026. Improves on the previous Grok Imagine Video generation pipeline.
  *Status: Released*

- **Gemini 3.5 Flash (GA) + Gemini 3.5 Pro (upcoming) — Google**
  Gemini 3.5 Flash went generally available on May 19 at Google I/O 2026 and is now the default model in the Gemini app and Google AI Mode in Search. API pricing: $1.50 / $9.00 per million tokens (input/output). Google confirmed Gemini 3.5 Pro is entering its June 2026 release window.
  *Status: Flash — GA; Pro — Imminent*

- **NVIDIA RTX Spark Superchip — NVIDIA**
  Announced at Computex 2026 on June 1. An Arm-based superchip designed to integrate AI agents, content creation, and gaming in a single portable PC form factor. Jensen Huang framed it as reinventing the PC around on-device AI.
  *Status: Announced / Hardware release TBD*

## Notable Ecosystem News

- **Apple Intelligence (iOS 27):** Tim Cook announced a Gemini-powered Siri alongside a multi-AI Extensions framework at WWDC 2026, giving iPhone users the ability to choose Claude (Anthropic) or other frontier models as their default assistant.
- **OpenAI government restrictions:** OpenAI is cooperating with a U.S. government request to limit its newest models (GPT-5.6 Sol, Terra, Luna) to trusted partners, raising questions about the future of open AI access and international competitiveness.
- **Microsoft × Mayo Clinic:** Joint initiative to co-develop a frontier AI model specifically for clinical healthcare workflows, signaling a push for domain-specific frontier models in high-stakes industries.
