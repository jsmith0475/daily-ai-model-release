# AI Model Releases - June 9, 2026

### Top Updates

- **Google Gemini 3.5 Flash (Google)** — A frontier-level multimodal model optimized for speed, running at approximately 4x the pace of comparable models. Now generally available (GA since May 19 at Google I/O 2026) and set as the default in the Gemini app and Google Search AI Mode. Pricing: $1.50 / $9.00 per million input/output tokens; 1M token context window. *Status: Released (GA)*

- **Google Gemini 3.5 Pro (Google)** — Announced at Google I/O (late May 2026) for June release; positioned as a step-up from Flash with stronger reasoning and task-completion performance. *Status: Imminent release (June 2026 window)*

- **Google Gemma 4 12B (Google DeepMind)** — Open-weight reasoning model showing a reported 2x improvement on hard multi-step tasks compared to its predecessor. Part of Google's ongoing open-source model family. *Status: Released*

- **Microsoft MAI Models — MAI-Code-1-Flash & MAI-Thinking-1 (Microsoft)** — Announced at Microsoft Build (June 2, 2026), these are Microsoft's first in-house AI coding and reasoning models. MAI-Code-1-Flash targets code generation with competitive efficiency; MAI-Thinking-1 is a reasoning model. Positioned to reduce developer reliance on OpenAI APIs. Seven new MAI models total. Also announced: Azure Cobalt 200 VMs with a 50% performance uplift for agentic AI workloads. *Status: Announced / releasing on Azure*

- **Anthropic Claude Opus 4.8 (Anthropic)** — Latest flagship model in Anthropic's Opus line, announced May 28. Scores 88.6% on SWE-bench Verified and 74.6% on Terminal-Bench 2.1, representing best-in-class software engineering and terminal task performance among evaluated models. *Status: Released*

- **NVIDIA Nemotron 3 Ultra 550B A55B (NVIDIA)** — A 550-billion-parameter open model with a 55B active parameter mixture-of-experts architecture, released June 4, 2026. Designed for enterprise reasoning, agentic, and research workloads. *Status: Released*

- **OpenAI GPT-5.3-Codex (OpenAI)** — Merges the Codex and GPT-5 training stacks for state-of-the-art code generation and reasoning. Reports ~25% faster inference than previous generation and sets new benchmark highs on coding evals. Now available on AWS in addition to OpenAI's own API. *Status: Released*

- **OpenAI Real-Time Audio Models (OpenAI)** — Three new real-time audio models targeting conversational agents, translation, and transcription use cases. Designed to make live voice interactions more viable for support, sales, and education applications. *Status: Released*

- **Alibaba Qwen 3.7 Plus (Alibaba)** — Updated entry in Alibaba's Qwen open-weight series, continuing competitive performance in multilingual and reasoning benchmarks. *Status: Released*

- **Apple Siri (Custom Gemini-Based Model) (Apple / Google)** — Apple's new Siri runs on a custom 1.2-trillion-parameter model built on Google's Gemini architecture, under a licensing deal reportedly worth ~$1 billion/year announced in January 2026. *Status: Released (shipping with Apple OS updates)*

### Market Context

New AI models are arriving roughly every 2 days. The June 2026 wave is characterized by:
- **Reasoning as standard** — virtually all frontier releases now include chain-of-thought or dedicated reasoning modes
- **Efficiency competition** — GPT-4-class performance is being delivered at significantly lower cost tiers
- **Big Tech in-housing** — Microsoft (MAI), Apple (Gemini licensing), and Google (Gemma open weights) all reducing third-party model dependency
- **Agentic infrastructure** — Azure Cobalt 200 VMs, OpenAI Codex, and Anthropic's agent roadmap all reflect a shift from chat to long-horizon task completion
