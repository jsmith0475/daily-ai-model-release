# AI Model Releases - July 6, 2026

## Top Updates

- **Claude Sonnet 5 — Anthropic**
  Anthropic's most agentic model to date, capable of autonomously using tools like browsers and terminals while delivering near-Opus 4.8 performance at substantially lower cost. Scores 63.2% on SWE-bench Pro and 80.4% on Terminal-Bench 2.1 (surpassing Opus 4.8's 74.6%). Features a 1M token context window.
  *Specs: 1M context window | $2/$10 per million tokens (in/out) | Released June 30, 2026*

- **GPT-5.6 Sol, Terra & Luna — OpenAI**
  OpenAI launched three variants of its next-generation GPT-5.6 family on June 26, with initial access restricted to ~20 trusted partner organizations following US government requests for oversight before broader availability. General availability expected mid-July 2026.
  *Status: Limited / Restricted beta — public launch delayed*

- **Gemini 3.5 Flash — Google**
  First model in Google's new Gemini 3.5 family, optimized for complex agentic workflows and coding tasks. Output tokens run 4x faster than competing frontier models, making it Google's speed-focused flagship.
  *Status: Released*

- **Grok 4.5 — xAI**
  xAI's latest Grok iteration, currently in private beta deployment at SpaceX and Tesla. No public benchmarks released yet.
  *Status: Private beta*

- **Nemotron 3 Nano Omni — NVIDIA**
  Open omni-modal reasoning model unifying vision, audio, and language in a single 30B-parameter mixture-of-experts architecture. Designed for on-device and edge deployment scenarios.
  *Specs: 30B params (MoE) | Status: Released / Open*

- **Nemotron 3 Ultra 550B — NVIDIA**
  NVIDIA's flagship open large-scale model in the Nemotron 3 family, targeting frontier-level reasoning workloads at scale.
  *Specs: 550B params | Status: Released / Open*

- **Claude Fable 5 — Anthropic**
  Anthropic's creative and narrative-focused model, released June 9, 2026. Positioned for long-form creative writing and storytelling applications.
  *Status: Released*

- **Gemini 3.5 Live Translate — Google**
  Real-time speech-to-speech translation model that automatically detects and translates across 70+ languages with live audio input.
  *Status: Released*

- **Microsoft MAI Models (7 new) — Microsoft**
  Microsoft AI unveiled a family of seven new in-house models developed under its Microsoft AI (MAI) initiative, described as part of a "hill-climbing machine" strategy for enterprise AI capability.
  *Status: Announced / Releasing*
