# AI Model Releases - April 16, 2026

> Daily briefing covering the most significant AI model launches, announcements, and updates from the past 24 hours.

## Top Updates

- **Alibaba "Happy Oyster" — Alibaba / ATH AI Innovation Unit**
  A new gaming-focused generative AI model released today that generates game videos and simulates real-world environments. Designed to compete directly with Tencent in the AI-powered gaming and content creation space.
  - **Availability:** Released (April 16, 2026)
  - **Source:** [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-04-16/alibaba-releases-new-ai-model-for-gaming-development) | [GuruFocus](https://www.gurufocus.com/news/8796411/alibaba-unveils-happy-oyster-model-for-aigenerated-game-videos)

- **Claude Opus 4.7 — Anthropic** *(imminent, expected this week)*
  Successor to Opus 4.6, optimized for multi-step reasoning, autonomous long-running tasks, and multi-agent coordination. Ships with improved alignment, reduced hallucinations, and tighter agentic guardrails. Accompanied by a new AI design tool integrated with Figma, and extensions into Microsoft Word and PowerPoint.
  - **Context window:** 1 million tokens
  - **Availability:** Announced / launching this week (April 14–17)
  - **Source:** [Dataconomy](https://dataconomy.com/2026/04/15/anthropic-to-launch-claude-opus-4-7-this-week/) | [TechBriefly](https://techbriefly.com/2026/04/15/anthropic-to-launch-claude-opus-4-7-and-new-ai-design-tool-this-week/)

- **Claude Mythos — Anthropic** *(restricted access)*
  Anthropic's most powerful frontier model to date, described as a "step change" above Opus 4.6. Excels at advanced reasoning, coding, and cybersecurity vulnerability detection. Due to its power in surfacing security exploits, it will NOT be publicly released — only accessible through the gated "Project Glasswing" program (~50 partner organizations).
  - **Scale:** ~10 trillion parameters
  - **Availability:** Restricted / Not public (announced April 7, 2026)
  - **Source:** [Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/digital/new-anthropic-ai-model-mythos-seen-exposing-vulnerabilities-in-banking-tech-infrastructure-95031.htm) | [The Print](https://theprint.in/opinion/ai-claude-mythos-us-india-act-now/2904114/)

- **NVIDIA Ising — NVIDIA** *(open model)*
  The world's first open AI model family designed to accelerate quantum computing. Delivers quantum error-correction decoding that is up to 2.5× faster and 3× more accurate than traditional methods. Aims to shorten the path to practical, fault-tolerant quantum computers.
  - **Availability:** Released (April 14, 2026) — open model
  - **Source:** [NVIDIA Newsroom](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-launches-ising-the-worlds-first-open-ai-models-to-accelerate-the-path-to-useful-quantum-computers)

- **Alibaba HappyHorse-1.0 — Alibaba / ATH AI Innovation Unit**
  A video generation model that topped blind-test leaderboards on Artificial Analysis for both text-to-video and image-to-video generation. Revealed to be an Alibaba product after going viral anonymously; now confirmed as part of Alibaba's broader video AI suite alongside Happy Oyster.
  - **Availability:** Released / Benchmarked (early April 2026)
  - **Source:** [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/04/10/alibaba-happyhorse-ai-video-model-benchmark-reveal.html)

- **Meta Llama 4 Scout & Maverick — Meta** *(open source)*
  Two new open-source LLMs released under the Llama 4 family. Scout features a massive 10-million-token context window; Maverick targets enterprise use with 1M tokens. Both use a Mixture-of-Experts architecture with 17B active parameters. Meta also released **Muse Spark**, its first proprietary (closed) AI model, on April 8.
  - **Scout:** 17B active params, 10M token context
  - **Maverick:** 17B active params, 1M token context
  - **Availability:** Released (April 5, 2026)
  - **Source:** [AI Models April 2026 Roundup](https://renovateqr.com/blog/ai-models-april-2026) | [LLM Stats](https://llm-stats.com/llm-updates)

- **Google Gemma 4 — Google DeepMind** *(open model)*
  Four model variants (2.3B → 31B parameters), all natively multimodal (text, image, video). The 31B Dense variant ranks #3 globally among open models on Arena AI. Represents Google's most capable open-weight release to date.
  - **Sizes:** 2.3B, ~9B, ~18B, 31B (Dense)
  - **Availability:** Released (April 2, 2026)
  - **Source:** [AI Models April 2026 Roundup](https://renovateqr.com/blog/ai-models-april-2026) | [Build Fast with AI](https://www.buildfastwithai.com/blogs/latest-ai-models-april-2026)

- **GPT-5.4 Update — OpenAI**
  A significant quality-of-life update to GPT-5.4 reduces refusals on benign edge-case requests by 40% while maintaining safety standards. Extends context-window handling and improves performance on multi-document analysis tasks. Shipped in three variants (Standard, Thinking, Pro).
  - **Availability:** Updated / Generally Available
  - **Source:** [LLM Stats](https://llm-stats.com/ai-news) | [OpenAI Release Notes via Releasebot](https://releasebot.io/updates/openai)
