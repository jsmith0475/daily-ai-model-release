# AI Model Releases - April 28, 2026

> Daily briefing covering the latest AI model launches and announcements from the past 24 hours.

---

### Top Updates

- **GPT-5.5 — OpenAI** *(Released)*
  OpenAI's latest flagship model delivers improved coding, computer use, and deep research capabilities. Rolling out to paid subscribers (Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise) via ChatGPT and Codex.

- **DeepSeek V4 Flash & V4 Pro — DeepSeek** *(Preview)*
  Two preview variants of DeepSeek's new flagship: V4 Flash (fast, low-cost) and V4 Pro (higher-capability). Both are mixture-of-experts models with **1 million token context windows**, trained on Huawei Ascend 950PR chips. Pricing: V4 Flash at $0.14/$0.28 per million tokens (in/out); V4 Pro at $0.145/$3.48 per million tokens — significantly undercutting competitors.

- **Gemini 3.1 Ultra — Google** *(Released)*
  Google's most significant model release this year. Features a **2 million token context window** with native multimodal support across text, image, audio, and video — designed from training to reason across all modalities simultaneously without transcription intermediaries.

- **Muse Spark — Meta** *(Announced)*
  Meta's first flagship LLM from its newly formed Superintelligence Labs (under Chief AI Officer Alexandr Wang). Delivers competitive multimodal perception, reasoning, health, and agentic task performance at a fraction of the compute cost of Llama 4. Meta also announced **$115–135 billion in AI capex** for 2026, nearly double last year.

- **Claude Mythos Preview — Anthropic** *(Preview, select partners)*
  Anthropic's new model with advanced cybersecurity capabilities, previewed to select enterprise partners. Full availability details pending.

- **Ising — NVIDIA** *(Announced)*
  The world's first family of open-source AI models purpose-built to accelerate quantum computing. Delivers up to **2.5× faster** and **3× more accurate** error-correction decoding compared to traditional approaches.

- **Gemma 4 — Google** *(Released, Open Source)*
  Four model variants released under the **Apache 2.0 license**, continuing Google's open-weight strategy and providing GPT-4-class performance for self-hosted deployments.

- **Luma Agents — Luma AI** *(Launched)*
  Built on Luma's Uni-1 model, this multi-agent system coordinates AI across text, images, video, and audio to generate end-to-end creative work autonomously.
