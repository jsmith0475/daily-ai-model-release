# AI Model Releases - April 18, 2026

### Top Updates

- **Claude Opus 4.7 (Anthropic)** — Released April 16; Anthropic's newest flagship targeting complex reasoning and long-running agent workflows. Follows the Sonnet 4.6 / Opus 4.6 family. *Status: Released*

- **Gemini 3.1 Ultra (Google)** — Features a 2-million token context window with native cross-modal reasoning across text, image, audio, and video (no transcription intermediaries). Ships with a sandboxed Code Execution tool allowing the model to write, run, and test code mid-conversation. *Status: Released*

- **Gemma 4 (Google / NVIDIA)** — Open-source multimodal model series (E2B, E4B, 26B, 31B parameter variants) under Apache 2.0. Co-optimized with NVIDIA for local/edge deployment on RTX PCs, DGX Spark, and Jetson modules. Designed for on-device reasoning, coding, vision, and audio. *Status: Released*

- **GPT-Rosalind (OpenAI)** — OpenAI's first Life Sciences model series entry; a frontier reasoning model designed to compress the 10–15 year drug discovery timeline. Available as a research preview in ChatGPT, Codex, and the API for qualified/trusted-access customers. *Status: Research Preview*

- **NVIDIA Ising** — World's first family of open-source AI models purpose-built to accelerate quantum computing error-correction decoding. Delivers up to 2.5× faster and 3× more accurate results versus traditional approaches. *Status: Released (open-source)*

- **Meta Muse Spark (Meta)** — Meta's first flagship LLM developed under its newly formed Superintelligence Labs (led by Chief AI Officer Alexandr Wang). Meta has committed $115–135B in AI capex for 2026. *Status: Announced*

- **Grok 4.20 Beta 2 (xAI)** — Current public flagship from xAI, listed alongside GPT-5.4, Claude Sonnet 4.6, and Gemini 3.1 Pro as the leading models as of early April 2026. *Status: Beta*

- **Huawei 950PR AI Chip** — Huawei's new inference-focused AI accelerator; ByteDance and Alibaba reportedly placing large orders. Positioned as a domestic alternative amid ongoing chip restrictions. *Status: Announced / Shipping*
