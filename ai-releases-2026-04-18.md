# AI Model Releases - April 18, 2026

### Top Updates

- **Claude Opus 4.7 — Anthropic** | Released April 16. Anthropic's newest flagship model targeting complex reasoning and long-running agentic workflows. Part of an expanded lineup that also includes Claude Opus 4.6 and Claude Sonnet 4.6. *Status: Released*

- **Gemini 3.1 Ultra / Pro / Flash-Lite — Google DeepMind** | Gemini 3.1 Ultra features a 2-million token context window with native multimodal understanding across text, image, audio, and video simultaneously (no transcription intermediaries). The lighter Flash-Lite variant is optimized for high-volume classification tasks. Google's new compression algorithm also cuts AI memory needs by 6×. *Status: Released*

- **Gemma 4 family — Google (co-optimized with NVIDIA)** | Open model family in E2B, E4B, 26B, and 31B parameter variants, tuned jointly with NVIDIA for local and edge deployment across RTX PCs, DGX Spark, and Jetson edge modules. *Status: Released*

- **GPT-5.4 family — OpenAI** | OpenAI's current lineup consolidates around four variants: GPT-5.3 Instant (fast everyday tasks), GPT-5.4 Thinking (deep reasoning), GPT-5.4 Pro (maximum capability), and GPT-5.4 mini (cost-efficient reasoning). GPT-4o has been fully retired from all ChatGPT plans as of April 3. *Status: Released*

- **Meta Muse Spark — Meta Superintelligence Labs** | Meta's first flagship LLM built under Chief AI Officer Alexandr Wang's newly formed Superintelligence Labs. Competitive on multimodal perception, reasoning, health, and agentic tasks at significantly lower compute cost than prior Llama 4 mid-size variants. *Status: Released*

- **NVIDIA Ising — NVIDIA** | World's first family of open-source AI models purpose-built to accelerate quantum computing. Delivers up to 2.5× faster and 3× more accurate quantum error-correction decoding vs. traditional approaches. *Status: Released / Open-source*

- **Apple Siri (reimagined) — Apple** | Completely rebuilt AI-powered Siri announced for 2026 debut. Context-aware, cross-app integration with "on-screen awareness." Apple has partnered with Google to use Gemini under the hood. *Status: Announced*
