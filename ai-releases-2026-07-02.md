# AI Model Releases - July 2, 2026

## Top Updates

- **Claude Sonnet 5 — Anthropic**
  The latest Claude flagship model, now the default for Free and Pro plans. Features significantly improved language reasoning and instruction following. Available via API at $2/M input tokens and $10/M output tokens (introductory pricing through August 31, 2026). Released June 30, 2026; **generally available**.

- **Claude Fable 5 & Mythos 5 — Anthropic**
  The U.S. Department of Commerce lifted export controls on these two powerful models effective July 1, 2026, with global access restoration beginning for Fable 5. Previously restricted under export regulations. Status: **access restored globally** (Fable 5) / rollout in progress (Mythos 5).

- **GPT-5.6 Family (Sol, Terra, Luna) — OpenAI**
  Three-model family announced June 26, 2026, targeting stronger reasoning, coding, and cybersecurity capabilities. Sol is the flagship; Terra and Luna serve speed and cost tiers respectively. Currently in **limited preview** with phased rollout planned.

- **Gemini 3.5 Flash — Google**
  Google's latest fast production model in the Gemini 3.x series, now **generally available** via the Gemini API and Google AI Studio and Android Studio. Designed for low-latency, high-throughput enterprise workloads.

- **Gemini 3.1 Flash Image & Gemini 3 Pro Image — Google**
  Two new image generation models released June 30. Flash Image targets fast creative workflows; Pro Image targets high-fidelity output. Both are **available now** via Google AI Studio and the Gemini API.

- **Gemma 4 12B — Google**
  Open-weights model that runs locally on consumer hardware with 16 GB RAM. Designed for on-device AI agents. **Released and open-source**.

- **MAI Model Family (7 models) — Microsoft**
  Microsoft AI unveiled seven in-house models including MAI-Thinking-1 (reasoning), plus specialized models for coding, image understanding, and transcription. Announced this week; availability details staggered by model. Status: **announced / rolling out**.

- **Grok 4.3 — xAI**
  Updated Grok model with reported improvements to reasoning and multimodal understanding. **Released** (exact date early July 2026).

- **Qwen 3.5 Series — Alibaba / Qwen Team**
  Lightweight open models at 0.8B, 2B, and 4B parameters (non-reasoning variants). Targeted at edge deployment and cost-sensitive inference. **Open-source release**.
