# AI Model Releases - 2026-05-30

## Top Updates

- **Gemini 3.5 Flash — Google**
  Generally available today via the Gemini API, Google AI Studio, and Android Studio. Delivers frontier-level intelligence at 4× the output-token speed of comparable models, outperforming Gemini 3.1 Pro on coding and agentic benchmarks (Terminal-Bench 2.1: 76.2%, MCP Atlas: 83.6%). Priced at $1.50/$9 per 1M tokens (input/output); 1M-token context window. **Status: Released (GA)**

- **GPT-5.3-Codex — OpenAI**
  Announced as OpenAI's most capable agentic coding model to date, combining the Codex and GPT-5 training stacks. Reported ~25% speed improvement over predecessors with new highs on coding benchmarks. Targets agentic software engineering workflows. **Status: Announced/Released**

- **Gemini Omni — Google** *(announced at Google I/O 2026, May 18–20)*
  A new model series combining Gemini's reasoning capabilities with native creation: accepts image, audio, video, and text input and can output video grounded in real-world knowledge. Positioned as a unified multimodal generation model. **Status: Announced**

- **GPT-5.5 Instant — OpenAI** *(launched May 5, now default)*
  Became the new default ChatGPT model as of early May. Optimized for low latency and lower cost compared to the GPT-5.4 family, while maintaining competitive quality for everyday tasks. **Status: Released (default)**

- **Grok 4.3 — xAI**
  A real-time web-connected reasoning model with tight integration into X (formerly Twitter) data streams and live web access. Designed for up-to-the-minute research and reasoning tasks. **Status: Released**

- **Composer 2.5 — Anthropic**
  Positioned as the lowest effective per-task cost among frontier-class models at launch: $0.10 uncached at 100K input / 20K output tokens. Targets high-volume agentic workloads where cost efficiency is critical. **Status: Announced**

- **Ising — NVIDIA**
  Open-source family of AI models purpose-built to accelerate quantum computing error-correction decoding. Claims up to 2.5× faster and 3× more accurate results compared to traditional approaches. **Status: Released (open-source)**

- **SubQ Subquadratic LLM — SubQ**
  The first commercial subquadratic-attention large language model, supporting a 12M-token context window. Represents an architectural departure from standard transformer attention, targeting long-context efficiency. **Status: Released**
