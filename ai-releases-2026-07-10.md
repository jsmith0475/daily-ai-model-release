# AI Model Releases - July 10, 2026

## Top Updates

- **Grok 4.5 — SpaceXAI (xAI)**
  Released July 8, 2026 and currently running in private beta with teams at SpaceX and Tesla. Built on a new V9 foundation with approximately 1.5 trillion parameters, offering substantially improved reasoning and multimodal capabilities over Grok 4.1.
  *Specs: ~1.5T parameters | Status: Private beta (SpaceX / Tesla)*

- **GPT-5.6 (Sol / Terra / Luna) — OpenAI**
  OpenAI's next-generation model family in three tiers: Sol (flagship), Terra (balanced), and Luna (fast/affordable). Currently gated to roughly 20 government-vetted partner organizations, with broader rollout expected in coming weeks. ChatGPT Work, an agent-based product powered by Codex and GPT-5.6, is publicly available.
  *Pricing: Sol $5/$30 · Terra $2.50/$15 · Luna $1/$6 per M tokens | Status: Limited government/partner access; ChatGPT Work available*

- **Claude Fable 5 — Anthropic**
  Returned to service July 1 after US export-control restrictions were lifted (model had been offline for ~3 weeks). Billing now requires usage credits at $10/M input and $50/M output tokens. Anthropic simultaneously launched **Claude Science**, a research workbench with 60+ preconfigured tools, and announced an internal drug discovery program targeting neglected diseases.
  *Pricing: $10/$50 per M tokens | Status: Available (usage-credit required)*

- **Gemini 3.5 Flash — Google**
  Fast, production-ready multimodal model positioned as the everyday workhorse of the Gemini 3.5 family. Available via Vertex AI and Google AI Studio for enterprise and developer use.
  *Status: Generally available*

- **Gemini 3.5 Pro — Google**
  Google's most capable frontier model remains in limited Vertex AI enterprise preview as of July 8 — now three weeks into delays — with no confirmed general availability date. Most anticipated frontier launch of July 2026.
  *Status: Limited enterprise preview (delayed)*

- **Gemini Nano Banana 2 Light (Image) & Gemini OVNI Flash (Video) — Google**
  Two generative-media models highlighted in early July. Nano Banana 2 Light generates images in approximately 4 seconds; OVNI Flash targets fast video synthesis. Both represent Google's push into sub-second-class media generation.
  *Status: Available (limited rollout)*

- **Chinese AI Models — Various**
  Chinese models (including offerings from Baidu, Alibaba, and ByteDance) now account for 30–46% of enterprise API token usage flowing through US developer platforms, exceeding 30% of all OpenRouter gateway tokens every week since February 2026. No single headline launch today, but the aggregate market share shift is the week's biggest competitive story.
  *Status: Ongoing market expansion*
