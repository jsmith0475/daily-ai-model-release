## AI Model Releases - April 23, 2026

### Top Updates

- **Claude Opus 4.7 — Anthropic** (Released April 16)
  Anthropic's new flagship, replacing Opus 4.6 as the default across all Claude products. Designed for complex reasoning and long-running agentic workflows with a new `xhigh` effort level.
  *Specs:* 1M token context window, 3.3× higher-resolution vision, 87.6% on SWE-bench Verified, 94.2% on GPQA. Pricing: $5 / $25 per M tokens (input/output).

- **Grok 4.3 Beta — xAI** (Released April 17)
  xAI's latest Grok iteration in beta, continuing the company's push into frontier reasoning and coding tasks. Full specs pending official announcement.

- **Gemini 3.1 Pro / Ultra — Google** (April 2026)
  Gemini 3.1 Pro leads 13 of 16 major benchmarks and ties GPT-5.4 Pro on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at roughly one-third the API cost. The Ultra variant ships with a 2M token context window and native multimodal reasoning (text, image, audio, video) plus a new sandboxed Code Execution tool — no transcription intermediaries.

- **Meta Llama 4 Maverick — Meta** (Released April 5)
  Top open-weight model of the month. A massive 400B-parameter mixture-of-experts architecture with a 10M token context window, now the leading open-source option for long-context tasks.

- **Google Gemma 4 — Google** (Released April 2)
  Frontier-level open-weight model released under Apache 2.0. Positions as the most capable openly licensed model from Google to date, suitable for on-device and self-hosted deployment.

- **Meta Muse Spark — Meta** (Released April 8)
  Meta's first flagship LLM beyond Llama branding, targeting multimodal perception, reasoning, health, and agentic tasks at significantly lower compute cost than comparable mid-size Llama 4 variants.

- **NVIDIA Ising — NVIDIA** (Announced April 14)
  World's first family of open-source AI models purpose-built to accelerate quantum computing. Delivers up to 2.5× faster and 3× more accurate quantum error-correction decoding vs. traditional approaches. Targets researchers and enterprises building fault-tolerant quantum processors.

- **GLM-5.1 — Zhipu AI** (Released April 7)
  Latest iteration of China's GLM series, continuing Zhipu AI's push into frontier open-weight Chinese LLMs with improved reasoning and multimodal capabilities.

### Notable: Not Released

- **Claude Mythos 5 — Anthropic** (Announced, withheld)
  A 10-trillion-parameter model that leaked in late March. Anthropic confirmed its existence on April 7 but announced it will **not** be publicly released due to cybersecurity risks. Access limited to select partners under "Project Glasswing."
