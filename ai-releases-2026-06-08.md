# AI Model Releases - June 8, 2026

### Top Updates

- **Google Gemini 3.5 Flash (Google)**
  The first model in Google's latest series combining frontier intelligence with fast inference; outperforms Gemini 3.1 Pro on challenging coding and agentic benchmarks while matching larger flagship models on general tasks. Available via the Gemini API at $1.50 / $9.00 per million input/output tokens; already the default model in the Gemini app and AI Mode in Search.
  *Status: Generally Available (GA since May 19, 2026)*

- **OpenAI GPT-5.3-Codex (OpenAI)**
  OpenAI's most capable agentic coding model to date, merging the Codex and GPT-5 training stacks; ~25% faster than its predecessor and sets new highs on key coding benchmarks. Represents a step-change from pure code generation to a general-purpose coding agent.
  *Status: Released*

- **Microsoft MAI-Code-1-Flash & MAI-Thinking-1 (Microsoft)**
  MAI-Code-1-Flash is Microsoft's inaugural in-house model, generating source code from natural-language descriptions. MAI-Thinking-1 is a companion reasoning model available in private preview via Microsoft Foundry. Both were announced at Microsoft Build in early June.
  *Status: MAI-Code-1-Flash released; MAI-Thinking-1 in private preview*

- **NVIDIA Ising (NVIDIA)**
  The world's first family of open-source AI models purpose-built to accelerate quantum computing, specifically for error-correction decoding. Delivers up to 2.5× faster and 3× more accurate results compared to traditional classical approaches.
  *Status: Open-source release announced*

- **Google Imagen 3 Nano & Pro (Google)**
  Image-generation models that accept video files as prompts to produce context-aware visuals such as thumbnails and infographics. Both tiers became widely available in June 2026.
  *Status: Generally Available*

- **OpenAI Real-Time Audio Models — 3 new models (OpenAI)**
  Three new real-time audio models targeting conversational agents, translation, and transcription use cases. Aims to make live voice interactions for support, sales, and education significantly more practical.
  *Status: Released*

- **Alibaba Qwen 3.7 Max (Alibaba)**
  Latest iteration of Alibaba's Qwen series; part of a broader wave of open and API-accessible LLM releases arriving roughly every three days in June 2026.
  *Status: Released*

- **Intel AI Innovations at Computex 2026 (Intel)**
  Intel announced new rackscale AI infrastructure solutions at Computex (June 2–5) targeting large-scale inference and agentic workloads, alongside industry-specific AI packages.
  *Status: Announced*
