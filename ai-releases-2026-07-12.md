# AI Model Releases - 2026-07-12

## Top Updates

- **GPT-5.6 (Sol / Terra / Luna) — OpenAI**
  Three-tier model family that became the default ChatGPT experience on July 9, 2026. Sol targets STEM/reasoning, Terra is positioned as the wide-enterprise tier (price-competitive with Claude Sonnet), and Luna is the lightweight option. Pricing: Luna at $1/$6, Terra at $2.50/$15 per 1M tokens (input/output). Status: **Generally Available**.

- **GPT-Live-1 & GPT-Live-1 mini — OpenAI**
  Full-duplex voice models released July 8 for more natural real-time conversation. Support simultaneous speaking/listening, natural interruption handling, and live translation. Status: **Released**.

- **ChatGPT Work — OpenAI**
  Agentic productivity product that merges Codex into the ChatGPT desktop app, shipping with a 15-integration plugin directory for enterprise workflows. Status: **Released**.

- **Grok 4.5 — xAI**
  Coding-focused model released July 8, trained with Cursor. Benchmarks: 83.3% on Terminal-Bench 2.1. Pricing: $2/$6 per 1M tokens. Positions as a cost-efficient coding model. Status: **Generally Available**.

- **Claude Sonnet 5 — Anthropic**
  Released June 30, 2026. Flagship for writing style, voice fidelity, and complex instruction-following. Features a 1M-token context window. Pricing: $2/$10 per 1M tokens. Status: **Generally Available**.

- **Muse Spark 1.1 — Meta**
  Meta's first paid AI model, released July 9. Pricing: $1.25/$4.25 per 1M tokens. Status: **Generally Available**.

- **Seedream 5.0 Pro — ByteDance**
  Multilingual text-and-layout image generation model with region-precise editing capabilities. Supports complex multilingual text rendering within images. Status: **Released**.

- **Gemini 3.5 Pro — Google DeepMind**
  Still in limited Vertex AI enterprise preview as of July 12 due to token efficiency and long-task reasoning gaps flagged by enterprise testers. General availability now expected ~July 17, 2026. Status: **Preview / Upcoming GA**.

## Other Notable Developments

- The White House is in advanced talks with OpenAI, Google, and Anthropic to finalize voluntary frontier AI standards covering benchmarks, testing timelines, and access rules.
- SK Hynix completed a $28–29B ADR listing on July 10 — the largest in history — reflecting surging demand for high-bandwidth memory that powers AI training and inference.
- Cloudflare launched granular AI bot management, letting site owners separately control Search, Agent, and Training crawlers.
