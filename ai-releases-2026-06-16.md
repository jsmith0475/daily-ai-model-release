## Top Updates

- **Claude Opus 4.8 — Anthropic** *(Released May 28, 2026 · Now #1 on leaderboard)*
  Anthropic's most capable model has dethroned GPT-5.5 on the Artificial Analysis leaderboard with a 61.4% blended score and 1545 Elo. Features adaptive thinking, an effort parameter for token efficiency, and up to 128K output tokens. Key benchmarks: SWE-bench Verified 88.6%, SWE-bench Pro 69.2%, GDPval-AA 1890.
  **Context window:** 1M tokens · **Pricing:** $5/M input, $25/M output · **Status:** Generally available

- **GPT-5.5 (incl. Pro & Instant variants) — OpenAI** *(Released April 23, 2026)*
  OpenAI's first fully retrained base model since GPT-4.5; natively omnimodal with state-of-the-art agentic performance. Variants include GPT-5.5 Pro (highest capability) and GPT-5.5 Instant (speed-optimized). Key benchmarks: Terminal-Bench 2.0 82.7%, FrontierMath Tier 1–3 51.7%, Tier 4 35.4%.
  **Status:** Generally available

- **OpenAI Real-Time Audio Models — OpenAI** *(June 2026)*
  Three dedicated real-time audio models targeting conversational agents, live translation, and speech transcription. Positioned for support, sales, and education use cases. OpenAI also launched a self-serve Ads Manager inside ChatGPT alongside these models.
  **Status:** Available (details on pricing TBA)

- **Gemini 3.5 — Google DeepMind** *(Announced Google I/O 2026, May 2026)*
  Introduced as part of Google's "agentic Gemini era," Gemini 3.5 delivers frontier intelligence for agents and coding workloads. Paired with Managed Agents in the Gemini API for orchestrated, multi-step task flows.
  **Status:** Available in Google AI Studio and Gemini API

- **Gemini 3.1 Pro — Google DeepMind** *(Preview released February 19, 2026)*
  Google's first use of a ".1" version increment (vs. the usual ".5"), signaling a focused intelligence upgrade. Strong multimodal capabilities across documents, code, images, and research files with very large context windows.
  **Status:** Preview / Generally available

- **NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 Content Safety — NVIDIA** *(June 2026)*
  Customizable multimodal safety model designed for global enterprise AI deployments. Allows organizations to tailor safety thresholds across modalities for compliance and localization requirements.
  **Status:** Available

- **NVIDIA Nemotron 3 Ultra 550B — NVIDIA** *(June 2026)*
  A 550B-parameter open model targeting enterprise and research inference workloads, with streaming ASR (Nemotron-3.5 ASR Streaming) variants also announced alongside it.
  **Status:** Available

- **MiniMax 3 & Qwen 3.7 — MiniMax / Alibaba** *(June 2026)*
  Both models are reshaping API pricing dynamics and open-source competitiveness in the June 2026 leaderboard landscape, rivaling proprietary alternatives on several benchmarks while enabling self-hosting and fine-tuning.
  **Status:** Available (open-weight)
