# AI Model Releases - May 15, 2026

## Top Updates

- **GPT-5.5 Instant — OpenAI**
  New default ChatGPT model with personalized search that can reference past conversations, uploaded files, and Gmail to tailor answers. Scored 81.2 on AIME 2025 and outperforms its predecessor on the MMMU-Pro multimodal reasoning benchmark.
  *Context window: not disclosed | Status: Available (Plus & Pro on web; mobile rollout pending)*

- **Gemini 3.1 Ultra — Google**
  Google's flagship multimodal model featuring a 2-million token context window with native understanding across text, image, audio, and video — no transcription intermediaries required. Tied with GPT-5.4 Pro at the top of the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index (57 pts).
  *Context window: 2M tokens | Status: Available*

- **Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite — Google**
  Efficiency-focused companion to Ultra, delivering 2.5× faster response times with a significantly lower price point.
  *Pricing: $0.25 per million input tokens | Status: Available*

- **Mistral 128B — Mistral AI**
  Mistral's new 128B-parameter flagship with async cloud coding sessions and a Work agentic mode built into Le Chat. Targets complex multi-step coding and enterprise agentic workflows.
  *Parameters: 128B | Released: May 3, 2026 | Status: Available*

- **GLM-4.7 — Zhipu AI**
  Chinese lab Zhipu AI's latest model, notable for being trained entirely on Huawei Ascend silicon (no NVIDIA dependency) and achieving a 1.2% hallucination rate — among the lowest reported for a frontier model.
  *Pricing: $0.11 per million input tokens | Status: Available*

- **Grok 4.3 — xAI (via OCI)**
  xAI's latest reasoning model is now available on Oracle Cloud Infrastructure Enterprise AI, with strong performance across advanced logic, math, coding, and multi-step analysis.
  *Status: Available (OCI Enterprise AI)*

- **NVIDIA Nemotron 3 Nano Omni — NVIDIA**
  Fully open-source multimodal model capable of reasoning across video, audio, images, and text in a single inference pass. Now available on OCI Enterprise AI.
  *Status: Released / Open source*

- **Anthropic "Dreaming" Agent Technique — Anthropic**
  A new agentic AI technique allowing autonomous systems to review their own prior behavior, identify patterns, and self-improve between sessions — without human intervention. Launched as a research preview.
  *Status: Research preview*
