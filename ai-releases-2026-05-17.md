# AI Model Releases - May 17, 2026

### Top Updates

- **Google Gemini 3.1 Ultra (Google)** — The most significant Google model release of 2026 to date, featuring a 2-million token context window with native multimodal support across text, image, audio, and video—no transcription intermediaries required. Continues the Gemini 3 lineage (~1T MoE architecture) with a 2× reasoning improvement on ARC-AGI-2 benchmarks. Status: **Released**

- **Google Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview (Google)** — A reasoning-focused variant of Gemini 3.1 with a 1M-token context window. Priced at $2.00 in / $12.00 out per 1M tokens. Status: **Preview / Beta**

- **Google Omni (Google)** — A new Gemini-based AI video generation system previewed ahead of Google I/O 2026. Supports generating, remixing, and editing video directly through conversational prompts. Status: **Announced**

- **Mistral 128B Flagship (Mistral AI)** — A 128-billion parameter flagship model launched May 3, 2026, with async cloud coding sessions and a new "Work" agentic mode inside the Le Chat interface. Targets enterprise coding and productivity workflows. Status: **Released**

- **Meta Muse Spark (Meta)** — Meta's first proprietary flagship LLM, competitive on multimodal perception, reasoning, health, and agentic tasks at significantly lower compute cost than comparable models. Status: **Released**

- **DeepSeek V4 Flash & V4 Pro (DeepSeek)** — DeepSeek's newest flagship series unveiled in April 2026, posting top-tier scores on coding benchmarks and major gains in reasoning and agentic capabilities—roughly one year after the original DeepSeek shook Silicon Valley. Status: **Released**

- **Thinking Machines "Interaction Models" (Thinking Machines / Mira Murati)** — From the startup founded by former OpenAI CTO Mira Murati, these "interaction models" are designed for real-time multimodal collaboration: continuously interpreting audio, video, and text simultaneously and responding dynamically. Status: **Announced**

- **GPT-5.5 (OpenAI)** — Released April 23, 2026 (six weeks after GPT-5.4), with major gains in agentic coding, computer use, and knowledge work. Reflects OpenAI's accelerated release cadence throughout early 2026. Status: **Released**
