# AI Model Releases - June 3, 2026

### Top Updates

- **Microsoft MAI-Code-1-Flash (Microsoft)** — Microsoft's debut in the coding-model space, generating source code for applications and websites from written natural-language descriptions. Announced at Microsoft Build 2026; availability details to follow from the conference.

- **Microsoft MAI-Thinking-1 (Microsoft)** — A new reasoning model released alongside MAI-Code-1-Flash, designed for high efficiency and performance at low token cost. Part of Microsoft's push to reduce dependence on third-party frontier models.

- **Google Gemini 3.5 Flash (Google DeepMind)** — Generally available as of today; outperforms Gemini 3.1 Pro on challenging coding and agentic benchmarks. Available through the Gemini API and Google's agent-first development platform, designed for frontier intelligence with action-oriented tasks.

- **OpenAI GPT-5.3-Codex (OpenAI)** — First model to combine the Codex and GPT-5 training stacks; reported ~25% speed improvement over prior versions with new benchmark highs. Now available on AWS in addition to OpenAI's own platform.

- **Anthropic Claude Opus 4.8 (Anthropic)** — Latest iteration of Opus, building on 4.7 with broad benchmark improvements; scores 84% on Online-Mind2Web for computer-use/agentic tasks. Released this week.

- **NVIDIA New Laptop AI Chips (NVIDIA)** — NVIDIA unveiled a powerful new laptop chip for Windows PCs, advancing the company's bid to own every layer of the AI stack from cloud to consumer edge. Announced June 2, 2026.

- **xAI Grok 4.20 (xAI)** — New release with multi-agent variants noted in the June 2026 AI launch wave; specific benchmark details and availability not yet fully published.

- **Mistral Codestral (Mistral AI)** — Coding-specialized model receiving attention in the June 2026 wave alongside other new releases; targets developers needing a lightweight, focused code-generation model.
