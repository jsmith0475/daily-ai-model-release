# AI Model Releases - 2026-05-13

> Daily briefing covering the past 24–48 hours of AI model releases and major announcements.

---

## Top Updates

- **DeepSeek V4 Pro** — DeepSeek (via Alibaba)
  Flagship open-source reasoning model with a massive 1 million token context window, designed for complex multi-step tasks and deep reasoning. Released May 12, 2026.
  **Specs:** 1M token context · $2.40 input / $4.80 output per 1M tokens · Open source
  **Status:** Released

- **DeepSeek V4 Flash (Non-Reasoning)** — DeepSeek (via Alibaba)
  Lightweight, cost-efficient counterpart to V4 Pro — optimized for speed and high-volume workloads where deep chain-of-thought is not required. Released May 12, 2026.
  **Specs:** 1M token context · $0.20 input / $0.40 output per 1M tokens · Open source
  **Status:** Released

- **Gemini Intelligence / Android Show announcements** — Google
  Google unveiled Gemini Intelligence as the new AI layer running underneath Android itself, shifting the OS toward an "intelligence system." Key features include cross-app agentic multi-step actions (e.g., extracting event data and booking via Expedia), Gemini in Chrome for Android with auto-browse, and form-filling assistance via Personal Intelligence. Announced at the Android Show: I/O Edition on May 12–13.
  **Status:** Rolling out (summer/fall 2026)

- **Googlebooks** — Google
  The first laptops designed from the ground up around Gemini Intelligence. Launching fall 2026 in partnership with Acer, Asus, Dell, HP, and Lenovo. Includes "Magic Pointer" (Gemini-enabled cursor) and native Android app compatibility.
  **Status:** Announced (fall 2026 launch)

- **OpenAI EU Cybersecurity Model** — OpenAI
  A specialized model rolled out for European markets focused on cybersecurity tasks, part of OpenAI's broader regional compliance and enterprise expansion strategy.
  **Status:** Released (EU)

- **GPT-5.5 Instant** — OpenAI *(past week)*
  New default ChatGPT model with personalized search across past conversations, files, and Gmail. Scored 81.2 on AIME 2025 (vs. 65.4 for predecessor) and 76 on MMMU-Pro multimodal reasoning (vs. 69.2). Released May 5, 2026.
  **Specs:** Improved AIME 2025: 81.2 · MMMU-Pro: 76 · Available to Plus/Pro users
  **Status:** Released

- **Grok 4.3** — xAI *(past week)*
  Released May 6 at an "aggressively low price" with a voice cloning suite and a new "Imagine" agent mode for creative projects. Tops the LM Arena leaderboard.
  **Status:** Released

- **Isomorphic Labs — $2.1B Funding Round** — Isomorphic Labs (DeepMind spinout)
  The AI drug discovery company raised $2.1 billion in new capital on May 13, signaling massive continued investment in AI-for-science applications.
  **Status:** Funding announcement
