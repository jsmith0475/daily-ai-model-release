## AI Model Releases - May 31, 2026

### Top Updates

- **GPT-5.4 — OpenAI**
  Combines the best advances in reasoning, coding, and agentic workflows; incorporates the coding capabilities of GPT-5.3-Codex while improving how the model works across tools, software environments, and professional tasks (spreadsheets, presentations, documents). Status: **Released**

- **GPT-5.3-Codex — OpenAI**
  OpenAI's most capable agentic coding model yet, merging the Codex and GPT-5 training stacks for best-in-class code generation, reasoning, and general-purpose intelligence. Approximately 25% faster than its predecessor and sets new highs on key coding benchmarks. Status: **Released**

- **Gemini 3.5 Flash — Google**
  Launched at Google I/O 2026 (May 19–20) and now broadly available via Google Antigravity, the Gemini API, Google AI Studio, and Android Studio. Delivers flagship-level intelligence at Flash-tier speeds; outperforms Gemini 3.1 Pro on challenging coding and agentic benchmarks. Status: **Generally Available**

- **Gemini Omni — Google**
  New model series that combines Gemini's reasoning capabilities with multimodal creation. Accepts image, audio, video, and text input and outputs video grounded in real-world knowledge. Status: **Released**

- **Claude Opus 4.8 — Anthropic**
  Released May 28, 2026. Features improved benchmark performance and enhanced honesty/alignment characteristics. Part of Anthropic's Claude 4.x family. Status: **Released**

- **NVIDIA Ising — NVIDIA**
  World's first family of open-source AI models purpose-built to accelerate quantum computing. Delivers up to 2.5× faster and 3× more accurate error-correction decoding compared to traditional approaches. Status: **Released (Open Source)**

- **Google Managed Agents (Gemini API) — Google**
  A single API call now provisions a remote Linux environment where an agent can reason, plan, call tools, execute code, manage files in an isolated sandbox, and browse the web. Status: **Released**

- **Anthropic "Dreaming" Agent Technique — Anthropic**
  New agentic AI technique designed to let autonomous systems review prior behavior, identify patterns, and improve future performance between sessions. Aimed at long-running managed agent workflows. Status: **Announced / Research Preview**
